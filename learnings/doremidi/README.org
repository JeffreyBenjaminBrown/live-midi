* The indicator light
The manual, "Steps for usage", "Connection": "The indicator
light flashes when the power is only supplied, and the
indicator light is always on when it is connected to the
computer."
** Flashing
The unit has power but no USB host has enumerated it: a
charge-only cable, a USB port or hub that passes power but not
data, or a wall adapter. 'tools/pedals/bridge.sh' then reports
"DOREMiDi MPC-20 (1a86:752d) not found".
** Steady
A computer has enumerated it as a USB device.
** After a firmware upgrade
The product page's firmware notes add that the light "flashes
slowly" for a few seconds after "Upgrade Completed", before
the unit is unplugged and reconnected.
