* 0. heads-up: a change already landed that may move the goalposts
=request.org= #3 changed the edo reflection from "active loop, *all-solid*" to
"*sounding* loop: dim backdrop, each note solid only while it sounds" -- a
deliberate new aesthetic, *no flashing*. Two deferred ideas below were written
*before* that and assume the old all-solid/flashing style, so they may want
rethinking rather than building as written. Flagging so we don't polish toward an
aesthetic we just left.

* 1. richer remap-mode LEDs
Today, in remap mode the edo grid shows the targets (or the group-transpose
center) *all solid*, and your loops-grid selection isn't highlighted back to you.
Deferred ideas:
- (a) *highlight the selection on the loops grid* while you pick (the chosen
  rows / columns / cells light brighter than the dim picker baseline).
- (b) *group-transpose center + octave-equivalents* shown center-solid with the
  equivalents *flashing* rather than all-solid.
*What I need:* (a) is unambiguous -- OK to just build it? (your eyes to confirm
brightness reads right). (b) conflicts with the no-flashing aesthetic from #0 --
do you still want flashing equivalents in remap mode, or the dim-backdrop /
solid-when-relevant style to match play mode now? Your call.

* 2. auto-octave-shift on remap entry
On entering remap the targets can sit off-grid or awkwardly placed. The idea: auto-
shift the play register on entry so the targets land in a comfortable region, then
restore on exit (the *restore* already happens; only the auto-shift-in is missing).
*What I need:* the rule. Candidates: (i) shift so the lowest target sits on row 0;
(ii) center the targets in the grid; (iii) leave it (drop this). Pick one.

* 3. non-uniform playhead on the loop display
The loop-display columns are *note-groups*, not uniform time, so a real-time
playhead would sweep across them non-uniformly.
*What I need:* do you want a moving playhead at all, and if so: (a) highlight the
current note-group *column* (simple, matches the display model), or (b) advance a
separate real-time pixel across a column (needs a sub-column scheme)? Or (c) skip.
