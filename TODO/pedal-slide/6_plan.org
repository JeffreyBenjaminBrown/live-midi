#+title: Pedal slide: what ought to have happened
#+date: 2026-07-20

The post-mortem plan Jeff asked for: diagnosis of the three observed bugs
(from 4_bugs-jeff-ran-into.org + the diffs in 5_diffs.org + 3_progress.org),
the deeper causes, the abstractions a rebuild should stand on, what the spec
left open, and a slice-by-slice walk-through plan for doing it right,
manually, with hardware checkpoints. The existing implementation
(cedc9ad + a16b6dc) has been REVERTED (Jeff's call, below); it now lives in
=5_diffs.org= and in git history, not in the tree. The walk-through below
still describes doing it right, manually, with hardware checkpoints, on the
now-clean tree.

* diagnosis of the three bugs

** bug 2 (the halving) and bug 3 (dead pedal after arrival): one disease
The pure map (=Segments= in pedal_slide.rs) is CORRECT -- endpoint-exact,
continuous, all 22 unit tests honest. The instrument still lands at half the
interval because of an IDENTITY hand-off, not math:

- The pedal thread drives voices BY KEY (=apply_slide_drives= looks up
  =d.voice= and, by design, "a drive whose voice has vanished is skipped").
- At the midpoint flip, the voice is RE-KEYED (drone key = its filed pitch,
  so re-filing home from H to T re-keys the voice), and that re-key is
  DEFERRED: the engine queues it (pedal thread) and the grid thread applies
  it on its next repaint.
- After the flip, the pairing's notion of the voice's key and the voice's
  actual key in the map diverge; every subsequent drive is "skipped". The
  voice freezes at whatever pitch the slew had reached at the flip -- the
  hysteresis band puts that at ~0.55 of the travel, i.e. audibly HALF the
  interval. 27/46 -> ~13.5/46; octave -> tritone. Bug 2, exactly.
- Bug 3 is the same disease at the next stage: by arrival, pairing state
  and voice state have parted ways (and the arrival/"kink gone" logic was
  written in a completion mindset -- see the VolumeReturn's =reverted=
  latch for the mindset in miniature), so nothing is left driving anything,
  and the old home is no longer anybody's endpoint.

The confidence here is high but not verified end-to-end: the fingerprint
(freeze at the hysteresis point, skip-on-missing-key, deferred re-key) all
sit in the diff. The walk-through should confirm with the logging slice
(below) before believing me completely.

** bug 1 (chords crossfade instead of gliding): the managed set was wrong
=match_targets= pairs EDIT-MODE voices only. A chord recalled under slide
mode brings its pitches in as TARGETS, but the PREVIOUS chord's sounding
voices (SurfaceChord-keyed, never in the edit set) are invisible to the
matcher -- so nothing pairs, every new pitch becomes a fade-IN and every old
voice a fade-OUT. A crossfade: precisely what Jeff heard. The vision's
words ("its voices all join the target set... target each as-yet untargeted
voice that's in edit mode") technically permit this reading, which is how it
survived two careful readings -- the spec never says a recalled chord's
VOICES become slidable. Jeff's intent obviously includes chord-to-chord
pitch glides; the spec gap is recorded below.

** diagnosis verification
Mechanically confirmed against the corpse, before reverting cedc9ad + a16b6dc
(=edo_surface/src/surfaces_runtime/tests.rs=,
=pedal_slide_bug2_mechanical_diagnosis_confirmation=). The test builds a real
sustained+edited voice at pitch H (= 50, via the real key handler), enters slide
mode, picks T = H + 27 (edo 46, fund 80 Hz -- T = 77, freq(T) = 255.263 Hz), then
simulates both threads: the pedal thread's real loop (=on_pedal= + =drives()= +
=synth::apply_slide_drives=, exactly as =pedal_volume.rs='s
=expression_pedal_loop=) sweeping the pedal fraction 0.0 -> 1.0 in ~0.01 steps,
with the grid thread's real repaint slice (=reconcile=, =freeze_slide_voices=,
draining =take_refiles=, then =note_moved= + =rekey_drone= per refile -- exactly
=mod.rs='s pedal-slide repaint) applied every 5th pedal step.

CONFIRMED. The assertion (audible freq == freq(T)) fails: the voice settles at
212.554 Hz, not 255.263 Hz -- step ~64.85, i.e. H + ~14.85, a fraction 0.550 of
the 27-step travel. That fraction is exactly MIDPOINT + HYSTERESIS_BAND (0.5 +
0.05 = 0.55) -- the flip threshold, not a coincidence. Stepping through what the
test actually exercises:

- The flip (crossing f = 0.55) fires inside =on_pedal= (pedal thread):
  =update_home= re-keys the PAIRING's =p.voice= from the H-keyed drone to the
  T-keyed drone immediately, and queues a =Refile= for the grid thread.
- On that call and every one following, until the next repaint, =drives()=
  reports the T-keyed voice -- but the actual =VoiceMap= entry is still H-keyed
  (the rename happens only when the grid thread's repaint drains the refile).
  Each of those drives is silently skipped by =apply_slide_drives= ("a drive
  whose voice has vanished is skipped"), so the H-keyed voice's
  =slide_freq_target= freezes at its last successfully-applied value: freq(H +
  0.55*27), from the last pedal step before the flip.
- The next repaint's =reconcile= runs BEFORE the refile is drained and
  =note_moved= updates the ring: it reads the ring's =Reason::Edit= set, which
  still reports the pitch as H (unchanged), while the pairing's own filed pitch
  (via the ALREADY-flipped =self.home=) now reads T. T is not in {H}, so
  =reconcile= treats the voice as having "left the edit selection" and CANCELS
  the pairing outright. =freeze_slide_voices= (called with the now-dropped,
  T-keyed voice) also misses -- the rename to T hasn't happened yet either -- so
  the frozen target is never even zeroed, just abandoned in place.
- With the pairing gone, =drives()= is empty for the rest of the sweep, so
  =apply_slide_drives= no-ops for every remaining pedal step (0.60 through 1.0).
  The voice's =slide_freq_target= never changes again; the render slew settles
  onto it and holds there -- audibly frozen just past the midpoint, not at the
  target.

So the diagnosis's headline claim (freeze near half the interval, driven by a
key-identity mismatch surviving the deferred re-key) is confirmed, and the
mechanism is sharper than "a drive silently misses a renamed key" alone (that by
itself is transient and self-corrects at the very next repaint): the transient
miss races a =reconcile= call that reads STALE ring state and permanently drops
the pairing before the re-key and the ring update land. The plan's illustrative
"~13.5/46" above was a rough half; the measured landing (~14.85/46, fraction
0.550) lands exactly on the hysteresis threshold instead, matching the more
precise prediction already in this diagnosis ("the hysteresis band puts that at
~0.55 of the travel").

* the deeper causes (ranked)

1. *A sliding voice's identity was allowed to change mid-flight, across
   threads, asynchronously.* One logical entity (the sliding voice) had its
   state split between the pedal thread (pairing, map) and the grid thread
   (key, ring filing, sink), with its IDENTITY (the pitch-keyed drone key)
   handed from one filing to another through a queue while both sides kept
   acting. Everything else wrong is downstream of this.
2. *"Which voices does the pedal manage" was conflated with "which voices
   are in edit mode".* Right for the single-note case that the vision
   opened with; wrong the moment chords arrived. The slide needs its OWN
   membership concept.
3. *Arrival semantics were never specified*, and the implementation
   defaulted to "completion" (the natural reading of "the kink is gone").
   Jeff's expectation -- arrival = role swap, the far side now holds the OLD
   home, the pedal lives forever -- is the better design and also the more
   literal reading of "'home' switches from one side to the other every
   time I reach it".
4. *Process: the pure core was proven and the wiring was not.* 770 green
   tests, all three bugs live. The unit tests pinned Segments; nothing
   pinned "pedal at 1 => the AUDIBLE frequency equals the target" through
   the real path (engine -> drives -> voice map -> render). And no hardware
   checkpoint existed between "empty" and "everything": two phases built
   the entire feature before the first human ear touched it.

* the abstractions a rebuild should stand on

- *StepPitch newtype* (fractional EDO steps) with explicit conversions to
  Hz and to cell/filed pitch. Kills the units-confusion class outright and
  makes "linear here = log-linear in Hz" a property of the type.
- *Stable voice identity for the duration of a slide.* Either (a) a slide
  voice's KEY never changes mid-flight -- filing/painting alias through a
  lookup (filed-pitch -> key) instead of re-keying, and any re-key happens
  only at slide END, in the thread that owns keys -- or (b) a VoiceRef
  handle (id-based, not pitch-based) that the map carries independently of
  filing. (a) is less machinery.
- *One owner for all slide mutation.* The pedal thread SENDS positions
  (already ~100 Hz); the grid thread owns the engine, the pairings, the
  re-filing, and the drives. Latency cost: one repaint cycle, inaudible
  under the 10 ms render slew (which stays). No queues of identity
  changes, no skipped drives, no cross-thread races by construction.
- *Role labels separate from map data.* Home/target/far are LABELS over a
  pairing whose map is just two anchored segments between two pitches.
  The midpoint flip relabels (and re-files/paints); it never mutates the
  map. Arrival relabels and collapses the map to one segment between the
  two pitches -- the old home is now the far side. Nothing is ever
  "completed" while slide mode is on.
- *A slide-managed set*, explicit and per grid: voices the pedal owns.
  Edit-mode voices join on pick; a chord recalled under slide moves its
  voices INTO the set (pairable); fade voices are born into it. Matching,
  fades, LEDs, and reconcile all read this set, not the edit set. (The
  edit set remains what the multipliers/=1 read -- unchanged.)
- *The wiring-level invariant harness*: a test fixture that runs engine +
  drives + voice map + a short render together and asserts, for scripted
  pedal traces: endpoint => audible pitch exact; no sample-to-sample jump
  beyond the slew's bound ANYWHERE (including across flip and arrival);
  after arrival, reverse reaches the old home exactly. Property-ish, cheap,
  and it would have caught all three bugs before any hardware did.

* what was ill-specified (for the spec's next revision)

- Post-arrival: what the far side holds (now settled: the old home; pedal
  never dies while mode is on).
- Slidable membership: what makes a voice pairable (settled direction: an
  explicit managed set; chords recalled under slide JOIN it -- their voices
  become the pairing side, not merely their pitches the target side).
- Whether a new pick after arrival replaces the far side (the old home) or
  chains somewhere else -- the home-proximity rule presumably answers it
  (pick replaces the side you are far from), but say it explicitly.
- Fade+slide coexistence on one voice (a fading-in voice that then gets
  paired for pitch): allowed? The rebuild should pick one rule.
- What the =1/multipliers do to a voice that is slide-managed but no longer
  edit-selected (possible once the sets diverge).

* the walk-through plan (thin slices, hardware checkpoint after each)

Rule for every slice: it ends with Jeff playing it. No slice builds on an
unverified predecessor. Each slice's "verify" list is what the ear must
confirm; the wiring harness grows alongside with the same assertions.

** slice 0: instrumentation
A dev logging switch that prints, per pedal event: fraction, each managed
voice's (key, map pitch, slewed audible pitch). Ten lines; every later
slice's disputes get settled by this instead of by guessing. (Also: confirm
the bug-2 diagnosis against the corpse before burying it.)
** slice 1: one voice, one target, no flip, no fades
Toggle + pick + the single anchored segment + the render slew + arrival as
ROLE SWAP. The managed set exists from day one (contains one voice). No
midpoint flip yet (roles swap only at endpoints), no LED subtleties beyond
target flash. Verify by ear: full travel reaches the target exactly; back
travel reaches the old home exactly; repeat indefinitely; no jumps.
** slice 2: mid-flight retarget (the kink)
Pick while between endpoints; far-side goalpost moves; kink at current
fraction; hysteretic reversal. Verify: no jump at pick; endpoints exact
both ways; the "catch-up" feel Jeff designed.
** slice 3: the midpoint flip -- labels, LEDs, filing only
Hysteresis band; colors swap; ring re-filing via the alias (NO re-key, or
key-owner-thread re-key -- whichever abstraction was chosen). Verify: LEDs
swap as specified; audible pitch provably unaffected by the flip (slice-0
logging); clears from both surfaces cancel cleanly mid-flight.
** slice 4: fades
The quartic anchored fades: single-pick swell (no edit voices), fade-out of
leftover voices. Verify: swell from true silence to sibling level; no
discontinuity when retargeting mid-fade; toggle-off settles fades as plain
drones.
** slice 5: chords
Chord recall under slide joins the chord's VOICES to the managed set and
its pitches to the targets; the matcher pairs across; extras fade. Verify:
chord-to-chord slide GLIDES pitch (bug 1's test, by ear); replacement
semantics; already-satisfied pitches stay put.
** slice 6: the volume return
The amendment's kinked x^4 return (this part of the corpse may actually be
correct -- VolumeReturn is self-contained; re-verify rather than rewrite).
Verify: no volume jump at mode exit; both directions live immediately;
ordinary taper resumes at the endpoints.
