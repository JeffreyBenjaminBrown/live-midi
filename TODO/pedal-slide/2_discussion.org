* DONE my formalization of the goalpost-moving -- confirm this is your intent
The vision's kink description resolves, I believe, into this invariant:

  At every instant, the pedal-to-pitch map is two log-linear segments
  JOINED AT THE PEDAL'S CURRENT POSITION: one from (F_now, P_now) down to
  (0, whatever side 0 holds), one up to (1, whatever side 1 holds).
  Moving the pedal follows the segment you are moving along; the segment
  BEHIND you is re-derived from wherever you now are.

Consequences worth saying out loud, because they follow from the invariant
rather than from anything you wrote explicitly:
- The map is HYSTERETIC: sweep up along a kinked map, reverse halfway, and
  the path down is a fresh straight segment to home -- not a retrace of the
  curve you came up on. Pitch is always continuous, and the endpoints are
  always exact (0 lands home, 1 lands target), but the middle is
  path-dependent. I believe that is not just acceptable but the point --
  it is the same trick as MIDI "catch-up" knob pickup, generalized.
- "Once the pedal reaches either endpoint, the kink is gone" falls out
  automatically: at an endpoint the two segments fuse into one.
- The same invariant, with amplitude segments instead of pitch segments,
  CURES the fade-map ill-definedness you caught yourself on (see next).

* DONE target-matching needs a tie-break rule
"Target each as-yet untargeted voice to the nearest available target" is
greedy and order-dependent: with voices A < B and targets X < Y placed so
both voices are nearest to X, who gets X depends on processing order (and
greedy can be globally worse than an optimal pairing). Everywhere else the
instrument breaks ties LOWEST-FIRST (the looper's rule, the drag rule). I
propose: process edit-mode voices in ascending pitch, each taking its
nearest remaining target, ties to the lower target. Deterministic, cheap,
matches house style. Same rule mirrored for the fewer-targets case to
decide who fades out (untargeted voices = the ones left over after the same
ascending pass). Confirm or supply your own rule.

* DONE the midpoint flip wants hysteresis
Home/target swap "when I cross the midpoint". A pedal RESTING near 0.5
(sensor noise, foot tremor) would flip home/target -- and the LED colors,
and which end future picks retarget -- many times per second. Propose a
band: flip only on crossing 0.5 +/- epsilon (say 0.05) in the direction
away from the current home. Costs nothing musically (the flip matters only
when you later pick or look), prevents the flicker. Also note the flip is
PURELY about (a) which end a new pick replaces and (b) the LED roles -- it
must not itself move any pitch (it doesn't, under the formalization, since
the map doesn't mention "home"; only picks and the pedal do).

* DONE set membership and LEDs during flight
A sliding voice's actual pitch is intermediate, usually on no cell. The
vision specifies the two ANCHOR cells' looks (home: lit + dance; target:
100 ms flash, no dance) and the midpoint color swap. Open: where does the
voice LIVE in the pitch-keyed sets (sustain/edit) during flight? Proposal
matching your color swap: it stays filed at HOME's pitch until the midpoint
flip, then re-files to the target pitch (ring's note_moved), so clears,
retriggers, octave reflections, and the dances all follow the cell that is
currently painted as "the note". The voice's own sounding-class reflection
(bright octave-equivalents) would then track the FILED pitch, not the
instantaneous glide -- I think lighting the true intermediate class would
smear meaninglessly across cells anyway. Confirm.

* DONE leaving slide mode (and entering it) mid-anything
Unspecified transitions, all need a rule:
- Toggle slide OFF mid-flight: voices freeze at their current (possibly
  in-between, microtonally off-grid) pitches? Snap to nearest of home/
  target? I propose: freeze exactly where they are (smoothness above all;
  they are sustained voices, a later drag can tidy them), and drop all
  targets/kinks.
- Toggle OFF returns the pedal to VOLUME duty: the pedal is physically at
  some position; naively the grid's volume jumps there. Propose catch-up
  pickup: volume stays at its pre-slide value until the pedal position
  crosses it, then tracks (the same trick yet again). Symmetrically,
  toggling slide ON should not yank pitch anywhere: slide starts with no
  targets, so the pedal is inert until the first pick -- good, but say
  whether the VOLUME it abandons stays frozen at its last value (my
  assumption) or resets to unity.
- Sustain-clear / editmode-clear during flight: clearing sustain ends
  sliding voices like any drone (fine); clearing edit deselects them --
  proposal: deselection also cancels their slide pairing (a slide needs its
  selection), voices freeze in place, targets unlight.

* DONE (probably) smoothness machinery: interpret "10 ms" as a time constant
"10 ms to transition between two adjacent pedal values" read literally as a
rate limit means a full-range sweep drags for ~1.2 s, and the per-step
pitch delta varies with the kink anyway. I intend a one-pole smoother on
the pedal-driven pitch (per voice, in the render, exactly like the volume
pedal's GAIN_SLEW_SECS), sized so adjacent-step transitions take ~10 ms --
fast sweeps stay proportionally fast, zipper stays gone. The existing
glide_per_sample machinery (walk-to-target-then-stop) is the wrong shape
for a continuous controller; this will be a sibling mechanism, not a reuse.

* DONE (probably) mundane implications, noted so they're deliberate
- While a grid's pedal is sliding, that grid has NO live volume pedal
  (fader and master still work). Inherent to the design.
- The mis-trimmed EX-P (reads ~35..127 until its MIN thumbwheel is fixed)
  will have its slide range compressed the same way its volume is today --
  hardware, not code.
- Log-linear pitch segments = interpolation in cents, matching how every
  glide in the engine already walks (multiplicative). The pedal's
  [1,119]->[0,1] normalization is reused as-is; the volume taper params
  (curve_*) do not apply to pitch.
- Retargeting only ever moves the FAR side's goalpost (the vision's
  home-proximity rule), so a pick can never yank the pitch you are
  currently nearest to -- nice property, preserved by the formalization.
- 100 ms target flash: I'll drive it from the shared dance clock (the
  vision permits it), so it can't drift against the other animations.

** DONE [claude] implemented
The mode-off return is now `pedal_slide::VolumeReturn` -- the SAME anchored-kink
`Segments` (x^4 shape) the slide fades use: two quartic segments joined at
`(F_now, V_frozen)`, one to `(0, 0)`, one to `(1, 1)`, so pushing OR pulling the
pedal changes the volume at once, no dead zone. On reaching either endpoint it
returns `None` and the pedal thread resumes the ordinary `PedalVolumeCurve`
taper (continuous -- the segment's endpoints are 0 and 1, as the taper's are).
The dropped catch-up-pickup code is gone. Covered by the pure-module tests
`the_volume_return_is_continuous_at_mode_exit_and_immediate_both_ways` and
`the_volume_return_reverts_to_the_ordinary_taper_at_an_endpoint`.
