#+title: Pedal slide: the by-ear checklist
#+date: 2026-07-20

The accumulating hardware checklist, one section per slice of =6_plan.org=. Jeff
plays down the list at the grids; anything that misbehaves gets a note under its
item and becomes the next session's work.

Everything here is also pinned by an automated wiring-level test (the harness in
=edo_surface/src/surfaces_runtime/tests.rs=, "pedal slide: the WIRING-LEVEL
invariant harness"), which runs the real key handler, the real engine step, the
real voice map and the real block renderer, and asserts the audible frequency.
So this list is not "does the code work" -- it is *does it feel right*, which is
the only question the harness cannot answer.

* how to run it
#+begin_src sh
pw-jack cargo run --bin midi_pulse -- 2-monomes_2-softsteps
#+end_src
The EX-P pedals need =tools/pedals/bridge.sh= running on the HOST (see
=tools/pedals/README.org=). One pedal reads ~35..127 until its MIN thumbwheel is
fixed, so its slide range is compressed the same way its volume is -- hardware,
not code.

*The on-screen readout* (the same window that shows BPM and the factored pulse)
now carries a line per grid:

: LOM  pedal 072.4  low +0013.45

- =pedal= is that grid's EX-P on its own =[0,120]= scale. =--= means that pedal
  has never sent a CC this session -- and while it says =--=, the engine is
  deliberately agnostic about which side is home.
- =low= is the LOWEST voice on that grid in 46-EDO steps from the tuning
  fundamental (0 = the fundamental, 46 = an octave above it), to two decimals --
  so a slide mid-flight, which sits between cells on no note at all, reads as the
  fractional thing it is.

Both change continuously when a slide is behaving, so a discontinuity is visible
as it happens rather than reconstructed from memory afterwards.

For more detail, =PEDAL_SLIDE_LOG=1= prints a line per pedal move: fraction, home
side, each managed voice's key, its map pitch, and the frequency actually
sounding. That is the intended way to settle any "is it the map or the wiring?"
question without guessing.

* the untouched pedal [FIXED, NOT YET PLAYED]
Jeff, after the first hardware session: "if I haven't used the pedal at all in a
session, it seems to be assuming a certain point, at which the pedal might not
be." Correct -- and it was the discontinuity. The published pedal position
started at 0.0, so the engine took the heel as home. With the foot actually near
the toe, the first CC to arrive found the map already stretched between the wrong
ends, and the pitch LEAPT to wherever that CC fell.

Now the engine commits to no home until the pedal first speaks, and the side it
speaks from becomes home. Measured on the old behaviour the pitch jumped 2.6 EDO
steps on the first reading and kept climbing; now it does not move at all.

A. *Cold start.* Restart the instrument and do NOT touch the pedal. The readout
   should show =pedal --= for both grids.
B. *The first move must be silent.* Edit a note, turn pedal slide on, pick a
   target -- all without touching the pedal. Now move the pedal a little. *The
   pitch must not jump*, however far from the heel your foot happened to be.
C. *That side is home.* From there, travelling AWAY from where the pedal started
   should slide toward the target and arrive exactly; coming back should return
   exactly. If your foot started near the toe, that means pushing toward the HEEL
   goes to the target -- which is correct, not backwards.

* the wrong-way fade [NEW, NOT YET PLAYED]
Jeff's design, after the first hardware session. Picking a target with the pedal
parked part-way along its travel forces a steeper gear ratio on that first sweep
-- unavoidably, given that the target must be exact at the far end and the press
must not move the pitch. The near side of the pick point therefore cannot slide.
It now holds the home pitch and fades out instead.

D. *The wrong way holds the pitch.* Park the pedal around the middle, edit a
   note, turn pedal slide on, pick a target. Now move the pedal AWAY from the
   target. The pitch must not move at all -- and the note should get quieter,
   quartically, reaching silence at that end of the travel.
E. *Home comes back exactly.* Wander around below the pick point, then return to
   it. The note should come back to full volume, at exactly its original pitch.
   The pick point does not follow your foot -- that is deliberate, and it is what
   makes the home pitch impossible to lose.
F. *Arriving hands volume back.* Sweep the right way to the target. On arrival
   the pedal should stop affecting volume altogether, and the whole travel should
   now be one clean line between the two pitches.
G. *The silent end reclaims the range.* Instead, go all the way to the SILENT
   end. Now sweep back: pitch should slide home -> target and volume 0 -> full
   across the whole travel, both arriving together at the target.

* slice 1 -- one voice, one target [NOT YET PLAYED]
The toggle is each grid's *bottom-left cell*, =[0,15]= (a free play cell on this
rig; it is the accrete clear on =2-monomes_kmss-drums=, which does not carry
this feature). Dim at rest, bright while on.

Setup for every item: strike a note, press the cell BELOW it to edit it (which
also sustains it), lift your finger. Then press =[0,15]=.

1. *The toggle lights.* Bottom-left goes bright; the grid's volume pedal stops
   changing volume.
2. *A pick does not sound.* With the pedal at the heel, press a free pitch
   somewhere above the edited note. Nothing should sound, and the edited note
   should NOT jump to it. The pressed cell should start flashing bright/off
   about five times a second.
3. *The toe reaches the target.* Push the pedal all the way down. The note
   should glide smoothly up and *land exactly on the pitch you picked* -- play
   that pitch on the other grid to check it in unison. (This is the bug from
   =4_bugs-jeff-ran-into.org=: it used to land about halfway.)
4. *The roles swap on arrival.* At the toe, the cell you picked should now be
   lit and dancing (it is the note), and the cell you came FROM should now be
   the one flashing.
5. *And back.* Raise the pedal to the heel: the note should glide back and land
   exactly where it started. (This is the "pedal goes dead on arrival" bug.)
6. *Indefinitely.* Rock the pedal heel-toe-heel-toe several times. Every arrival
   should be exact, both ways, forever -- the pedal never "finishes".
7. *No jumps anywhere.* Move the pedal fast, slow, and reverse it mid-travel.
   The pitch should never step or click, including right at the moment the
   colours swap.
8. *Retarget mid-flight.* With the pedal halfway, pick a DIFFERENT free pitch.
   The sounding pitch must not budge at the moment you press. Continuing to the
   toe should land exactly on the new pitch; reversing to the heel should land
   exactly on the original one.
9. *Toggling off freezes.* Halfway through a slide, press =[0,15]= again. The
   note should hold its (probably between-cells) pitch, and the pedal should
   stop affecting it. Slice 6 is what makes the volume hand-back graceful; until
   then expect the volume to jump when you next move the pedal.
10. *Clearing cancels.* Mid-slide, press the editmode-clear (=[12,0]= or its
    pedal). The note should stay sounding, frozen where it was, and its target
    should stop flashing.

* slices 2 + 3 -- the kink and the midpoint flip [NOT YET PLAYED]
Slice 2 (mid-flight retarget) arrived with slice 1 -- see item 8 above, which is
its whole checklist. Slice 3 moves the home/target swap from the ENDS of the
pedal's travel to its midpoint, which is what =1_vision= specifies.

11. *The colours swap halfway.* Start a slide and push the pedal slowly past the
    middle. Somewhere just past halfway the two cells should trade looks: the
    target stops flashing and starts being lit + danced, and the pitch you came
    from starts flashing instead. *The sound must not change at all* at that
    moment -- the swap is about labels, not pitch.
12. *And back on the way down.* Coming back, they should trade again slightly
    BELOW halfway (there is a deliberate dead band, so the swap points differ
    going up and coming down -- that is intended).
13. *A resting foot does not flicker.* Park the pedal right around the middle and
    let your foot tremble. The colours must stay put, not strobe.
14. *Chaining slides -- the point of the whole thing.* Raise the pedal into pitch
    A. Now pick pitch B and LOWER the pedal into it. Then pick C and raise into
    that. Each pick should set the end you are heading FOR, and each travel
    should arrive exactly. This is the workflow =1_vision= opens with.
15. *With a finger still down.* Strike a note, edit it, and *keep your finger on
    it*. Pick a target and slide past the middle, then lift the finger. The
    slide must continue and still arrive exactly. (Lifting hands the voice from
    its cell to its pitch -- a hand-off with the same shape as the original bug.)
16. *Retriggering mid-slide.* Mid-slide, press the cell the note is currently
    lit at. It should re-strike (envelope only) and *keep sliding*.

* slice 4 -- the swell [NOT YET PLAYED]
With *nothing in edit mode*, a pick no longer does nothing: it makes the pedal a
swell into that pitch, from silence. (Your call in =2_discussion=: "let's go
with your favorite... it will definitely have its musical uses".)

17. *Swell into a pitch.* With pedal slide on, nothing edited, and the pedal at
    the heel: press any free pitch. Nothing should sound at all. Now push the
    pedal down -- the note should rise out of true silence, gently at first
    (it is an x^4 curve, so most of the loudness arrives in the last third).
18. *It is a real note.* At the toe it should be a normal sustained note: it
    lights, it square-dances, the handles and clears reach it.
19. *It flashes until it arrives.* The cell should flash while you are swelling
    it in, and stop flashing once it is fully in.
20. *Swell started mid-travel.* Park the pedal halfway, then pick. The note
    should be silent at that moment (no click, no jump) and swell in as you push
    on from there -- and still reach full exactly at the toe.
21. *The one you flagged.* =2_discussion=: swelling a note in, then picking a
    SECOND one while past the midpoint, means the second one takes the near side
    as home and only swells when you sweep back the other way. You predicted
    this would be confusing and wanted to try it anyway -- this is the item where
    you find out.

* slice 5 -- chords [NOT YET PLAYED]
A chord slot pressed while pedal slide is on does NOT recall the chord. Its
pitches become the target set, and whatever is already sounding glides into
them. The slot lights solid to say which chord you are aimed at.

22. *Chord to chord GLIDES.* Recall a chord normally (slide off). Turn pedal
    slide on, then press a DIFFERENT slot. Nothing should change yet. Now sweep
    the pedal: the sounding chord should *bend* into the stored one, every voice
    gliding. (This is bug 1 from =4_bugs-jeff-ran-into.org=: it used to
    crossfade -- old notes fading out while new ones faded in, no pitch movement
    at all.)
23. *No doubling.* Three voices in, three voices out. Aiming at a chord must not
    spawn a second copy of it.
24. *A bigger chord.* Aim a 2-note chord at a 4-note one: two voices should
    glide, and the two spare pitches should swell in from silence as you sweep.
25. *A smaller chord.* The reverse: the leftovers should fade out as you sweep.
26. *Already-satisfied pitches stay put.* Aim at a chord that shares a pitch
    with what is sounding. That voice should not wander off to a different
    target and come back.
27. *Aiming again.* Press a different slot mid-sweep. The new chord takes over
    as the target set with no jump.

* slice 6 -- the volume return [NOT YET PLAYED]
Toggling pedal slide OFF hands the pedal back to volume through the same kinked
x^4 construction the fades use, so it is live immediately in both directions.

28. *No jump at mode exit.* With the pedal parked mid-travel, toggle pedal slide
    off. The volume should NOT leap to wherever the pedal physically is.
29. *Live in both directions at once.* From there, push the pedal a little: the
    volume should rise straight away. Pull it back past where it was: it should
    fall straight away. Neither direction should have a dead patch.
30. *The taper resumes at the ends.* Run the pedal to either end and then use it
    normally. From that point on it should feel exactly like the ordinary volume
    pedal again.

* known rough edges (not bugs -- recorded in 3_progress.org)
- Toggling pedal slide off while a voice is *mid-swell* hands that voice back to
  the grid volume, so its loudness ramps (over ~20 ms, no click) to wherever the
  volume pedal now says. The pedal is uniform once slide mode is off; there is
  nowhere else for a half-swelled voice to go.
- If a note gets struck and sustained ON the pitch a slide is heading for, the
  slide still arrives correctly but that note keeps painting at the cell it came
  from until the collision clears. Nothing dies. Neither =1_vision= nor
  =2_discussion= considers this case -- see the question in 3_progress.org.
