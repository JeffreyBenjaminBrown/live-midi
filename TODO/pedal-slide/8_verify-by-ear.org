#+title: Pedal slide: the by-ear checklist
#+date: 2026-07-20

The accumulating hardware checklist, one section per slice of =6_plan.org=. Jeff
plays down the list at the grids; anything that misbehaves gets a note under its
item and becomes the next session's work.

Everything here is also pinned by an automated wiring-level test (the harness in
=edo_surface/src/surfaces_runtime/tests.rs=, "pedal slide: the WIRING-LEVEL
invariant harness"), which runs the real key handler, the real engine step, the
real voice map and the real block renderer, and asserts the audible frequency.
So this list is not "does the code work" -- it is *does it feel right*, which is
the only question the harness cannot answer.

* how to run it
#+begin_src sh
pw-jack cargo run --bin midi_pulse -- 2-monomes_2-softsteps
#+end_src
The EX-P pedals need =tools/pedals/bridge.sh= running on the HOST (see
=tools/pedals/README.org=). One pedal reads ~35..127 until its MIN thumbwheel is
fixed, so its slide range is compressed the same way its volume is -- hardware,
not code.

Set =PEDAL_SLIDE_LOG=1= in the environment to get a line per pedal move:
fraction, home side, each managed voice's key, its map pitch, and the frequency
actually sounding. That is the intended way to settle any "is it the map or the
wiring?" question without guessing.

* slice 1 -- one voice, one target [NOT YET PLAYED]
The toggle is each grid's *bottom-left cell*, =[0,15]= (a free play cell on this
rig; it is the accrete clear on =2-monomes_kmss-drums=, which does not carry
this feature). Dim at rest, bright while on.

Setup for every item: strike a note, press the cell BELOW it to edit it (which
also sustains it), lift your finger. Then press =[0,15]=.

1. *The toggle lights.* Bottom-left goes bright; the grid's volume pedal stops
   changing volume.
2. *A pick does not sound.* With the pedal at the heel, press a free pitch
   somewhere above the edited note. Nothing should sound, and the edited note
   should NOT jump to it. The pressed cell should start flashing bright/off
   about five times a second.
3. *The toe reaches the target.* Push the pedal all the way down. The note
   should glide smoothly up and *land exactly on the pitch you picked* -- play
   that pitch on the other grid to check it in unison. (This is the bug from
   =4_bugs-jeff-ran-into.org=: it used to land about halfway.)
4. *The roles swap on arrival.* At the toe, the cell you picked should now be
   lit and dancing (it is the note), and the cell you came FROM should now be
   the one flashing.
5. *And back.* Raise the pedal to the heel: the note should glide back and land
   exactly where it started. (This is the "pedal goes dead on arrival" bug.)
6. *Indefinitely.* Rock the pedal heel-toe-heel-toe several times. Every arrival
   should be exact, both ways, forever -- the pedal never "finishes".
7. *No jumps anywhere.* Move the pedal fast, slow, and reverse it mid-travel.
   The pitch should never step or click, including right at the moment the
   colours swap.
8. *Retarget mid-flight.* With the pedal halfway, pick a DIFFERENT free pitch.
   The sounding pitch must not budge at the moment you press. Continuing to the
   toe should land exactly on the new pitch; reversing to the heel should land
   exactly on the original one.
9. *Toggling off freezes.* Halfway through a slide, press =[0,15]= again. The
   note should hold its (probably between-cells) pitch, and the pedal should
   stop affecting it. Slice 6 is what makes the volume hand-back graceful; until
   then expect the volume to jump when you next move the pedal.
10. *Clearing cancels.* Mid-slide, press the editmode-clear (=[12,0]= or its
    pedal). The note should stay sounding, frozen where it was, and its target
    should stop flashing.

** things slice 1 deliberately does NOT do yet
Don't report these as bugs:
- The home/target colours swap only at the *ends* of the pedal's travel, not at
  the midpoint. The midpoint flip is slice 3.
- Picking a pitch with NOTHING in edit mode does nothing at all. The swell-from-
  silence is slice 4.
- Recalling a chord while pedal slide is on behaves as it always did. Chords are
  slice 5.
- Toggling pedal slide off hands the pedal back to volume bluntly. Slice 6.
