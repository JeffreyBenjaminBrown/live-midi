#+title: Pedal slide: rebuild progress
#+date: 2026-07-20

The build log for the rebuild specified in =6_plan.org=. (The file of this name
that logged the FIRST, reverted implementation went away with it; the diffs it
described survive in =5_diffs.org=.) Structured per =when-to-stop.org=.

* changes to the plan's envisioned order
** the hardware checkpoints are BATCHED, not blocking
=6_plan.org= says "Rule for every slice: it ends with Jeff playing it. No slice
builds on an unverified predecessor." Taken literally that means stopping after
slice 1 and waiting -- which =when-to-stop.org= forbids ("do not stop... a
milestone is not a reason"), and Jeff's instruction this session was to execute.

So: the slices stay exactly as planned -- separate, serial, one commit each,
each with its own wiring-level tests -- but the ear checkpoints accumulate in
=8_verify-by-ear.org= for one hardware session rather than gating each other.
What the rule was PROTECTING (never again a mega-phase whose failure is
undiagnosable) is preserved by the commit-per-slice structure: if slice 4
misbehaves at the grids, slices 1-3 are still independently verifiable and the
bisect is one commit wide.

The substitute for Jeff's ear in the meantime is the wiring harness, and it was
checked the only way a harness can honestly be checked: *the corpse's disease
was reintroduced deliberately* (confirm the identity hand-off without moving the
voice-map entry) and 4 harness tests failed, including the exact
"lands near half the interval" assertion. Then it was reverted and all 573 pass.

* what's done and went well
** slice 0 -- instrumentation
=PEDAL_SLIDE_LOG=1= prints, per engine step, the pedal fraction, which side is
home, and each managed voice's (key, map pitch, audible Hz). Rate-limited to
actual pedal movement (the repaint loop runs at ~1 kHz). Ten lines, in
=mod.rs::pedal_slide_log=.

** slice 1 -- one voice, one target, arrival as role swap
Toggle at each grid's =[0,15]=, the target pick, the anchored single segment,
the render slew, and arrival-as-role-swap. Green, and the harness pins the three
things the ear would check: endpoint => audible pitch exact; no discontinuity
anywhere including across the swap; after arrival, reversing reaches the old
home exactly.

*** the fix for all three original bugs is ONE structural change
The engine is a *plain owned field on =GridThread=*, not shared state behind a
lock. The grid thread already owns the ring, the sink and the held map; giving
it the engine too makes "one owner for all slide mutation" (=6_plan.org=) a fact
about the types rather than a discipline. The expression-pedal thread can no
longer reach the engine at all: it publishes its normalized position into an
atomic and that is its entire pedal-slide duty. The reverted build's ranked
cause 1 -- "a sliding voice's identity was allowed to change mid-flight, across
threads, asynchronously" -- is now unrepresentable.

*** identity moves only on confirmation
=on_pedal= never mutates a pairing's key or filed pitch. It PROPOSES =Refile=s;
the owner applies each to the ring, the held map and the sink, and only then
calls =confirm_refile=. A refile the owner declines leaves the pairing exactly
as it was. So the engine can only ever learn of a move that actually landed --
which is the general form of the specific bug, not just a patch for it.

* what's imperfect about what's done
- *The re-file is declined when its destination pitch is occupied* (something
  was struck and sustained at the target between the pick and the arrival). The
  flight continues correctly and audibly; only the FILING lags, so that one note
  paints at the cell it came from until the collision clears. Nothing dies,
  which was the alternative (=HashMap::insert= would have silently killed the
  occupant). Neither =1_vision= nor =2_discussion= considers this case -- see
  the questions section below.
- The =Kind= enum has one variant (=Pitch=) until slice 4 adds the fades, and
  =Shape::Quartic= is proven by unit test but unused until then. Deliberate: the
  shapes were lifted whole from =5_diffs.org= because that math was never the
  bug, and splitting them across slices would have meant re-deriving them.
- Toggling pedal slide off hands the pedal back to volume bluntly (the next CC
  jumps the volume to the pedal's physical position). Slice 6.

* choices determining the future
** the drone's KEY still moves with its filed pitch (deviating from 6_plan)
=6_plan.org= recommended option (a): "a slide voice's KEY never changes
mid-flight -- filing/painting alias through a lookup". I did the opposite, and
deliberately: *drone key == filed pitch* is an invariant the entire runtime
already relies on (=sustain_key=, =end_drones_at=, =cut_sustained=,
=glide_sustained_to=, the chord save). An alias would have broken that invariant
globally, for every caller, to serve one rare case -- more machinery, not less.

The reason (a) looked necessary was that re-keying had to cross a thread
boundary. It no longer does: the re-key, the ring re-file and the held-map
update are three statements in one function in the thread that owns all three.
That is the same thing the existing pitch-DRAG path does, and it has never had
this class of bug.

** the pedal-slide slew is its own field, not a reuse of the glide
=slide_freq_target= + =voices::SLIDE_SLEW_SECS=, a sibling of the =grid_gain=
slew. I tried to avoid the new field by reusing =freq_target= /
=glide_per_sample= (which would have touched no shared engine code and no ~25
test literals), and it does not work: sized for a live controller, that walk's
per-sample RATIO lands within a few f32 epsilons of 1.0, so small pedal
increments round to no motion and the pitch stair-steps -- exactly the zipper
the smoother exists to remove. =2_discussion='s "DONE (probably)" reading was
right, and now it is right for a recorded reason.

** StepPitch, a newtype for fractional EDO steps
=6_plan.org= asked for it and it earns its keep in one specific place: fractional
steps and Hz are both =f32=, and confusing them is a silent detune rather than a
compile error. =Segments= itself stays units-free (it carries amplitude for the
fades), so the newtype appears only at the boundary where the confusion lives.
