#+title: Pedal slide: rebuild progress
#+date: 2026-07-20

The build log for the rebuild specified in =6_plan.org=. (The file of this name
that logged the FIRST, reverted implementation went away with it; the diffs it
described survive in =5_diffs.org=.) Structured per =when-to-stop.org=.

All six slices are built. The ear checklist is =8_verify-by-ear.org=; nothing
here has touched hardware yet.

* changes to the plan's envisioned order
** the hardware checkpoints are BATCHED, not blocking
=6_plan.org= says "Rule for every slice: it ends with Jeff playing it. No slice
builds on an unverified predecessor." Taken literally that means stopping after
slice 1 and waiting -- which =when-to-stop.org= forbids, and the instruction
this session was to execute.

So the slices stayed exactly as planned -- serial, one commit each, each with its
own wiring-level tests -- but the ear checkpoints accumulate in
=8_verify-by-ear.org= for one hardware session rather than gating each other.
What the rule PROTECTED (never again a mega-phase whose failure is
undiagnosable) is preserved by the commit-per-slice structure: if slice 5
misbehaves at the grids, slices 1-4 are still independently verifiable and the
bisect is one commit wide.

The substitute for Jeff's ear was the wiring harness, checked the only way a
harness can honestly be checked: *both original bugs were deliberately
reintroduced, and the tests failed.* Reverting the drone re-key (bug 2's identity
hand-off) failed 4 tests, including the exact "lands near half the interval"
assertion. Restricting the chord matcher's candidates to edit-mode voices (bug
1's membership error) failed the chord test on "every one of them is a PITCH
slide, not a fade". Both were then restored and all 588 pass.

** slice 2 arrived inside slice 1
The mid-flight retarget is intrinsic to =pitch_pairing=, which was lifted whole
from =5_diffs.org= because that math was never the bug. Writing the blunt
"replace the pairing" version the slice-1 brief called for would have meant
writing a worse one first and then deleting it. Slice 2 instead contributed the
wiring-level test it lacked (reverse after a big retarget, still land home
exactly).

* what's done and went well
** slice 0 -- instrumentation
=PEDAL_SLIDE_LOG=1= prints, per engine step, the pedal fraction, which side is
home, and each managed voice's (key, map pitch, audible Hz). Rate-limited to
actual pedal movement. Ten lines, =mod.rs::pedal_slide_log=.

** the fix for all three original bugs is ONE structural change
The engine is a *plain owned field on =GridThread=*, not shared state behind a
lock. The grid thread already owns the ring, the sink and the held map; giving it
the engine too makes "one owner for all slide mutation" (=6_plan.org=) a fact
about the types rather than a discipline. The expression-pedal thread cannot
reach the engine at all: it publishes its normalized position into an atomic, and
that is its entire pedal-slide duty. The reverted build's ranked cause 1 -- "a
sliding voice's identity was allowed to change mid-flight, across threads,
asynchronously" -- is now unrepresentable.

** identity moves only on confirmation
=on_pedal= never mutates a pairing's key or filed pitch. It PROPOSES =Refile=s;
the owner applies each to the ring, the held map and the sink, and only then
calls =confirm_refile=. A refile the owner declines leaves the pairing exactly as
it was. The engine can only ever learn of a move that actually landed -- the
general form of the bug, not a patch for the one instance of it.

That generality paid immediately. The midpoint flip (slice 3) turned up *two more
places where a slide-managed voice changes key*, neither of which the reverted
build handled and neither of which is mentioned in =1_vision= or =2_discussion=:

- a finger LIFTING off a note being slid (=SurfaceFinger= -> =SurfaceDrone=, via
  =sustain_note=), and
- a finger LANDING on a sliding drone to retrigger it (the reverse).

Both are bug 2's disease by another route: the pairing would go on addressing a
key the voice map no longer had, and the note would freeze mid-glide. Same
discipline fixes both (=rekey_voice=, called by the owner after it moves the
voice), and both are pinned by tests through the real key handler.

** bug 1 was a MEMBERSHIP bug, not a matching bug
The reverted build asked the EDIT SELECTION which voices the pedal managed. That
is right for the single-note case =1_vision= opens with and wrong the moment
chords arrive, because a recalled chord's voices are seq-keyed and never in the
edit set. So the managed set is now explicit, and each pairing records WHY it is
managed (=Tenure=): =WhileEdited= (a pick -- deselecting cancels it, per
=2_discussion='s "a slide needs its selection") or =WhileSounding= (a chord voice
or a swell -- the edit selection never had a say, so it does not get one now).
=reconcile= reads that instead of assuming everything answers to the edit set.

** the wiring harness
Runs the real key handler, the real engine step, the real voice map and the real
block renderer, and asserts on the AUDIBLE frequency (and, for the swell, on
rendered peak amplitude -- "silent" should mean the ear hears nothing, not that a
gain field says zero). Its sweep helper checks every intermediate step for
discontinuity, so "no jumps anywhere, including across the role swap" is asserted
~100 times per test rather than at the endpoints.

* what's imperfect about what's done
- *A re-file is declined when its destination pitch is occupied* (something got
  struck and sustained at the target between the pick and the arrival). The
  flight continues and arrives correctly; only the FILING lags, so that note
  paints at the cell it came from until the collision clears. Nothing dies, which
  was the alternative -- =HashMap::insert= would have silently killed the
  occupant. See the question below.
- *Toggling slide off mid-swell* hands that voice back to the grid volume, so its
  loudness ramps (over =GAIN_SLEW_SECS=, ~20 ms -- no click) to wherever the
  volume pedal now says. Once slide mode is off the pedal is uniform again and
  there is nowhere else for a half-swelled voice to go, but it is a rule nobody
  chose deliberately.
- *The pedal thread itself is untested* end to end: it needs the MPC-20 bridge
  and real hardware. What is tested is everything downstream of the atomic it
  publishes into, which is where all three bugs lived.
- The =2-monomes_kmss-drums= rig still cannot carry this feature: its =[0,15]= is
  the accrete CLEAR. =2_discussion= flagged this and it remains true and unbuilt
  -- that rig has no =pedal_slide_toggle= window, so nothing collides today.

* choices determining the future
** the drone's KEY still moves with its filed pitch (deviating from 6_plan)
=6_plan.org= recommended option (a): "a slide voice's KEY never changes
mid-flight -- filing/painting alias through a lookup". I did the opposite,
deliberately: *drone key == filed pitch* is an invariant the entire runtime
relies on (=sustain_key=, =end_drones_at=, =cut_sustained=,
=glide_sustained_to=, the chord save). An alias would have broken that invariant
globally, for every caller, to serve one rare case -- more machinery, not less.

Option (a) looked necessary only because the re-key had to cross a thread
boundary. It no longer does: the re-key, the ring re-file and the held-map update
are three statements in one function in the thread that owns all three. That is
exactly what the existing pitch-DRAG path does, and it has never had this bug.

** the pedal-slide slew is its own field, not a reuse of the glide
=slide_freq_target= + =voices::SLIDE_SLEW_SECS=, a sibling of the =grid_gain=
slew. I tried to avoid the new field by reusing =freq_target= /
=glide_per_sample= (which would have touched no shared engine code and no ~25
test literals), and it does not work: sized for a live controller, that walk's
per-sample RATIO lands within a few f32 epsilons of 1.0, so small pedal
increments round to no motion and the pitch stair-steps -- exactly the zipper the
smoother exists to remove. =2_discussion='s "DONE (probably)" reading was right,
and now it is right for a recorded reason.

** StepPitch, a newtype for fractional EDO steps
=6_plan.org= asked for it, and it earns its keep in one specific place:
fractional steps and Hz are both =f32=, and confusing them is a silent detune
rather than a compile error. =Segments= itself stays units-free (it carries
amplitude for the fades), so the newtype appears only at the boundary where the
confusion lives.

** the chord LAYER is untouched by an aimed-at chord
A slot pressed under slide mode does not =begin_recall=: no live registry
entries, no =active= flag. Those describe voices that EXIST, and under slide mode
a stored chord contributes only pitches. The slot lights through the pedal-slide
engine instead. This keeps chord storage's invariants intact, and it means a
future "recall while sliding" (if you ever want the chord to sound AND be the
target) is an addition rather than an unpicking.

** TODO what should happen when a slide's target pitch gets occupied mid-flight?
You pick a target, and before the slide arrives you strike and sustain a note ON
that pitch. Two notes now want the same drone key. I chose: *the slide still
arrives and sounds correctly, but stops re-filing* -- so it keeps painting at the
cell it came from until the collision clears. Nothing dies. The alternatives were
to overwrite (killing the note you just played) or to cancel the slide (killing
the gesture). Neither =1_vision= nor =2_discussion= considers the case.

** TODO should the =1 / multipliers reach a slide-managed voice that is no longer edit-selected?
=6_plan.org= listed this as ill-specified. The sets can diverge now (a chord
voice or a swell is managed without being edited). I left the multipliers reading
the EDIT set exactly as they always have -- so they do not reach those voices.
That is the no-change answer and it keeps one meaning for "what the multipliers
act on", but you may want the pedal's managed set to count as a selection.
