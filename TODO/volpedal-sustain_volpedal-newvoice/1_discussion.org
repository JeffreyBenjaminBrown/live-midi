*** setup

*** the handle
Hold a note and press the cell directly below it. The press should make no
sound. Lift the note: it should keep ringing. Press the same cell below again:
it should stop. The cell directly above a sounding note should just play.
*** the pedals
Channel 1 is the new-voice pedal and channel 2 the sustained one. If they're
backwards, swap the two =channel= values in the rig. Build a quiet bed with the
sustained pedal low, then play over it with the new pedal high. Sweeps should
be smooth on both. A note you sustain should drop to the bed's level as you
sustain it, not when you lift your finger.
*** the buttons
Bottom row, x = 6, 7, 8: OFF, ON, TOGGLE. TOGGLE starts bright, meaning ON is
momentary. Tap TOGGLE and it dims; ON then latches.
*** two grids
The pedals act on both grids. The buttons, timbres and volume keys act on their
own grid only.
** ONGOING confirm the one musical rule I chose: membership decides the pedal
*** the rule
A voice follows the sustained pedal while its pitch is in its grid's sustained
set, and the new pedal otherwise. So a note moves to the sustained pedal the
instant you sustain it, by its handle or by ON, even with your finger still
down. Lifting the finger afterwards changes nothing. Re-striking a sustained
pitch re-articulates it at the sustained level. A note struck while ON is live
is born at the sustained level. OFF, or the handle of a held sustained note,
hands it back to the new pedal. Every move is a 20 ms glide.
*** why I chose it
The change happens at the moment you deliberately sustain, and that also gives
the press audible confirmation. Lifting a finger stays an inaudible act, as it is
on a piano with the damper pedal down. While you build a bed with ON held, you
hear each note at the level the bed will have.
*** the alternative
"Finger decides": a voice follows the new pedal while any finger holds it and
the sustained pedal only once it rings alone. A sustained note would then change
level when you lift it, and re-striking a sustained pitch would play it at the
new-voice level. It's a small change if you prefer it. Answer under this
headline.

** what changed in the code
*** role pedals
An =[[expression_pedals]]= entry may now name =voices = "new"= or
=voices = "sustained"= instead of a =monome=. Such a pedal drives a new per-voice
gain factor, =role_gain=, slewed like the old grid pedal's. It needed its own
factor because in these compact rigs the volume keys already own the grid
pedal's. The pedal thread re-aims every live voice at its role's pedal on every
poll, so no key path needs a hook. A fresh note is born already at its role's
level, which avoids the unity-then-slew spike from the volume-spike bug. The
new validation rules: exactly one of =monome= or =voices=, each role at most
once, no role pedals beside chord or loop blocks, and no grid pedal on a monome
with a =volume_delta=.
*** handle sides
=edo_note_grid= takes optional =edit_handle= and =sustain_handle=. Left out,
other rigs keep edit-below, sustain-above. A monome with a momentary chord
block still has no handles, and setting them there is an error.
*** volume keys without chords
A grid's tone controls used to switch on only beside a momentary chord block.
Now any =volume_delta= switches them on.
*** the momentary start
=needs_holding= takes =initially_on = true=. Without it, a switchable bank
still starts latching, as before.
*** the calm surface
The two-timbres rig suppressed the square dance and the off-screen octave
hints, and used 150 ms status flashes, by testing for its chord block. Those
tests now ask whether the grid is one of the compact family (volume keys,
momentary chords or compact loops), so this rig looks like its sibling.
*** docs
=rigs/README.org= documents role pedals, handle sides and =initially_on=.
=ORIENTATION.org= has a section for this rig.

** glossary
*** sustained set
The pitches a grid keeps ringing without fingers. Each grid has its own. The
handle, ON and OFF all edit it.
*** drone
The fingerless voice that sounds a sustained pitch once its finger lifts.
*** role pedal
An EX-P pedal bound to a kind of voice on every grid (=voices= in the rig)
rather than to one grid (=monome=).
*** handle
The neighbouring play cell that, while a note sounds, acts on that note instead
of sounding.
*** onset role
The pedal a new voice starts under: sustained if its pitch is already sustained
or ON is live, else new.
*** slew
The 20 ms glide by which a voice's gain chases its target, so pedal sweeps and
role changes don't click or zipper.
