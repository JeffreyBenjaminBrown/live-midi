#+title: layout-thoughts -- windows on the two monomes, and the loops-monome crowding
Written for Jeff to make the one open C5c decision ("No room for timbre and looper").
Coordinates are =rect = [x0, y0, x1, y1]=, inclusive, x = column, y = row, (0,0) =
top-left. Both grids are 16x16.

* The two monomes and why each editor wants the OTHER one
6_plan 2.2: you edit the timbre for X on the *not-X* monome, so X stays visible while
you tweak it. So the design wants:
- the *live-timbre editor* on the *loops* monome (the edo play grid stays free to finger);
- the *loop-timbre editor* on the *edo* monome (the loop display stays visible).
The loop-timbre editor on edo is built and fine (see below). The live-timbre editor on
*loops* is where the trouble is.

* Window inventory -- EDO monome
| window              | kind               | rect             | size                | purpose                       |
|---------------------+--------------------+------------------+---------------------+-------------------------------|
| edo-grid            | edo_note_grid      | [0, 0, 15, 15]   | 16x16 (whole grid)  | the pitch play surface        |
| edo-shift-pad       | edo_shift_pad      | [13, 14, 15, 15] | 3x2 (lower-right)   | shift the pitch image         |
| loop-timbre editor* | timbre_editor loop | [0, 0, 15, 6]    | 16x7, folds to 16x1 | edit the active loop's timbre |
(* added by this feature; occludes the top of the play grid.)

The edo monome is essentially ONE big uniform window (the pitch field) plus a tiny
shift pad. Occluding the top 7 rows is cheap: you still have a 16x9 pitch field below to
finger, and the shift pad slides the pitch image so any pitch is still reachable. 6_plan
2.7 accepts this ("the grid is big"). Folded, the editor costs only row 0.

Unlike the edo grid, the loops monome is a *dense mosaic of small, function-specific
windows*. Every cell does a distinct job, and they fill the grid: the top 4 rows are the
slots + transport + copy/undo/remap, and the bottom 12 rows are the loop display. There
is essentially no empty space.

* The problem
Drop the 16x7 live-timbre editor onto the loops monome at [0,0,15,6] and it *buries the
entire control cluster*:

#+begin_example
LOOPS, live editor UNFOLDED at [0,0,15,6]:   E=editor  D=display  (controls gone)
       0123456789012345
   0   EEEEEEEEEEEEEEEE   <- editor covers ALL of: 16 slots, record/stop/play,
   1   EEEEEEEEEEEEEEEE      copy, undo, remap-mode -- every looper control is now
   2   EEEEEEEEEEEEEEEE      unreachable.
   3   EEEEEEEEEEEEEEEE
   4   EEEEEEEEEEEEEEEE   <- and the top 3 rows of the loop display too
   5   EEEEEEEEEEEEEEEE
   6   EEEEEEEEEEEEEEEE
   7   DDDDDDDDDDDDDDDD   <- the display reflows to here (top = editor.bottom + 1)
   .   ................      so it shrinks from 16x12 to 16x9
  15   DDDDDDDDDDDDDDDD
#+end_example

Why this is fine on edo but not on loops:
- On *edo*, occluding the top is harmless -- the play grid is interchangeable pitch keys,
  and the shift pad keeps every pitch reachable. You lose nothing essential.
- On *loops*, the occluded cells are *unique controls*. Burying "record" or "play" or the
  slots breaks the looper. They are not interchangeable, and there is no shift pad to slide
  them out from under the editor.
- Even *folded* (1 row), the editor's strip sits on row 0 and covers slots 0-3.

6_plan 5 sketches the loops monome with *only* the live editor + a reflowing display, and
says nothing about where the slots/transport/copy/undo go. That silence is the decision:
to host a live editor on the loops monome, the looper's own controls must be relocated,
shrunk, or dropped -- a user-facing change to a surface you already play.

* The reflow itself already works
The C5c *mechanism* is built and unit-tested: a loops-monome editor + the loop display
anchored at =top = "editor.bottom + 1"=, recomputed via the relayout resolver, so folding
the editor moves the display (verified: display top = 1 folded, 7 unfolded). The only
missing piece is a *config* that places everything without collisions -- i.e. the layout
decision above.

* Options (my read)
- (a) *Minimal / folded-resting.* Put the live editor's 1-row strip somewhere and accept
  that *unfolding* transiently hides the controls under it (you fold back to play). Shifts
  the looper down by one row. Least disruption; the looper stays intact.
- (b) *Redesign the loops grid.* Editor on top [0,0,15,6]; move slots + transport +
  copy/undo into the bottom rows; the reflowing display takes the middle. Cleanest editor,
  but relocates controls you know by muscle memory.
- (c) *Drop the loops-monome live editor.* The edo editor can already be =target=live= (it
  edits the live timbre when there's no selection), so live editing already works on one
  surface. The reflow stays as a tested engine for any future anchored window.

I lean (a): it keeps the working looper and still gives a live editor. But this is your
instrument and your muscle memory, so it's your call. Tell me which and I'll write the
config + verify it on the mock rig.
