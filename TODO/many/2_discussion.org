*** DONE and a real problem with pressure on a *held* button
This one matters more than the choice above. The decoder measures pressure like a
*drum*, not like a *switch*:

#+begin_src
onset (sum-of-4 > on_sum) -> Fire{pressure}
  ... for attack_ms (14 ms) keep watching -> Revise{pressure} on a higher peak
  ... then the pressure is FROZEN for the rest of the press
release (sum falls below off_sum) -> Release
#+end_src

So "how hard" = *the peak within the first 14 ms of the strike*. That measures how
fast you *stomp*, not how hard you are *standing*. For a drum that's exactly right.
For your *momentary accrete* -- a button you hold down for seconds while you play
-- it's the wrong quantity: press it gently and then lean, and it stays classified
"light" forever.

So if light/hard lands on a *momentary* control (accrete), the decoder needs to
keep tracking sum-of-4 for the life of the press and report *sustained* pressure,
not just the attack peak. That's a real (though not large) change to =decode.rs=,
and it's a new event kind alongside =Fire=/=Revise=/=Release=. If light/hard lands
only on *tap-like* controls (==1=, factor buttons), the existing attack-peak is
perfect and nothing changes.

That is a good reason to prefer ==1= over accrete for the first pressure pad.
