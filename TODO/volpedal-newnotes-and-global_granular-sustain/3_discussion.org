This supersedes the pedal parts of =1_discussion.org= and =2_discussion.org=.
** what the pedals do now
Each grid has its own pedal. It drives every note a finger holds on that grid,
sustained or not. A strike starts at its grid's pedal level. When the finger
lifts from a sustained note, nothing happens: the note keeps the level it had,
and later pedal moves don't reach it. Striking that pitch again gives a new held
note at the pedal's current level. The volume keys, master volume and everything
else multiply with it, as before.
** the rig
Grid A's pedal is MPC-20 channel 1 and grid B's is channel 2, each written in its
grid's section as =monome = "a"= (or ="b"=) plus =voices = "new"=. Swap the
channels if the cabling is the other way round.
** the code: two plugs per pedal
A pedal now has two independent plugs. =monome= picks the grid and =voices=
picks which voices on it: ="all"=, the old grid volume pedal and still the
default, or ="new"=, held notes only. A new-notes pedal without a =monome=
drives every grid, so "per grid" versus "per rig" is a rig edit now, not a code
change. Two pedals on the same grid's new notes, or =voices = "sustained"=, are
load errors. Lifting a finger touches nothing.
** the wiring map
=rigs/README.org= has a new section, "The wiring map". It lists every widget
connection that is a plug in the rig file, and every one that is still fixed in
code, so the next rewiring request starts from a list instead of a code search.

* [Claude] done
** the pedals
Channel 1 is the new-note pedal: each note starts at its level and keeps it.
Channel 2 is the global pedal: a live level over every note, held or sustained.
A note's volume is start level times global level, times everything else. Both
taper from silent at the heel to unity at the toe.
** the buttons
I read "far-left" as the bottom-left, x = 0 and 1 on the bottom row, since the
top-left already holds the timbre and volume keys. x = 0 opts the grid into the
new-note pedal, x = 1 into the global one. Lit means listening; both start lit.
Opting out of the new-note pedal makes later notes start at unity and changes
nothing sounding; opting out of the global pedal glides this grid's notes to
unity. Move them by editing their =rect= in the rig.
** the plugs
A pedal's job is now =role=: ="grid_volume"= (the old per-grid pedal, still the
default), ="new_note_volume"= or ="global_volume"=. The opt-in buttons are a new
widget, =pedal_toggle=, with =role=, =controls= (which grid; default its own) and
=initially_on=. The wiring map in =rigs/README.org= lists them.
