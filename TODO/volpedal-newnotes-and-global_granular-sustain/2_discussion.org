** DONE the pedal switch now waits for the finger
*** what you hear
A note follows the new pedal for as long as a finger holds it, sustained or not.
When the finger lifts from a sustained note, the note glides over 20 ms to the
sustained pedal's level. Striking a sustained pitch again gives a new note at
the new pedal's level, which returns to the sustained level when you let go. ON
works the same way: notes you play while it is live stay at the new level until
you lift them.
*** what changed underneath
The rule is now simpler than the one it replaces. A voice held by a finger
follows the new pedal and a fingerless drone follows the sustained pedal. The
one moment a note changes pedal is when its finger lifts, and the code re-aims
it right there. The pedal thread no longer sweeps every voice on every poll; it
re-aims only when a pedal moves.
** DONE tests read the rig instead of pinning it

editmode-clear button from the rig's layout. Their target pitches are always
