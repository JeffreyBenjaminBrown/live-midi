* Deviations from the plan's "how"
** The runtime's two exhaustive role matches
'settings.rs' 'resolve_settings' and the matching test in
'surfaces_runtime/tests.rs' match on 'PedalRole' exhaustively,
so the new variant had to be placed there for the crate to
compile. The plan gives the role its 'PedalTarget::
StringParameters' in slice 2; here both return 'None' for it
(the test's '.map' became '.filter_map'), so the parameters
pedal stays out of the pedal map until slice 2. No rig has such
a pedal yet, so nothing observable changes.
** The energy-pedal helper generalized without a rename
'validate_string_energy_pedal' keeps its name and its
energy-only rule (at most one per rig) and then calls a new
'validate_rig_wide_strings_pedal', which holds the shared
checks (no monome, no taper knobs, a 'cpal_strings' play sink).
The parameters role calls the shared one directly. The order
of the energy checks moved: "a second energy pedal" is now
checked before "names a monome"; both still fail.
** Small additions
- 'PedalRole::rig_spelling', for messages that name a role as
  the rig spells it.
- 'play_grids_sink_id' and 'play_grids_strings_params' in
  'rig.rs': the first was the inline lookup in the energy
  helper; the second feeds 'validate_pedal_targets'.
- 'TABLE_REBUILDING_PARAMETERS' in 'pedal_targets.rs': the five
  refused floats, used by the error message (it gives the
  reason) and by the every-field test.
- 'StringsPedalTarget' has a hand-written 'PartialEq' (key,
  min, max; the key fixes the setter), since comparing function
  pointers is unreliable; 'Debug' and 'Copy' are derived.
- 'StringsPedalTarget::value_at(position)' holds the one
  'min + (max - min) * position'.
- 'PedalTargetsPerChannel', the type alias for
  '[[Option<StringsPedalTarget>; MAX_PEDAL_TARGETS];
  NUM_PEDALS]' that 'LiveParams' will carry.
