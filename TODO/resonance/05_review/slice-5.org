* Deviations from the plan's how
** a pub 'play_grids_sink' rather than a pub params function
The harness needs the sink's sample rate and amplitude as well
as its parameters, so 'rig.rs' gains 'pub fn
play_grids_sink(&Rig) -> Option<&SinkRig>', and the private
'play_grids_strings_params' is now built on it. The harness and
the targets' validation choose one sink by one function.
'play_grids_strings_params' stays private: nothing outside
'rig.rs' needs it.
** without '--rig', channel 1 is the energy pedal
The slice 2 correction calls the no-rig default "no targets and
no energy pedal". Taken literally, '--pedal' and 'pedal_swell'
would put no energy in without a rig, where they always have,
and the plan says '--pedal' keeps its meaning. So the rigless
rendering has no targets and 'string_energy_pedal = Some(0)'. A
test pins it.
** the fallback sink
A rig with no play grid falls back to its first 'cpal_strings'
sink, as the harness always did. A rig whose play grids play a
non-strings sink is now an error in the harness; before, it took
the first strings sink anywhere in the rig.
** the tests live in the harness binary
Three tests in 'strings_harness.rs': the squeal grows and stays
below the clamp; every scene renders under the rig to finite
audio below the clamp; the rigless channel 1 pedal still swells.
Together they take about 4 s in debug. 'every scene' is a test
here, not only a manual run.
** the mock run's name
The helper passes the rig's name as the run's 'what' (used only
in the error message), and names the mock grids after the rig.

* Numbers moved
** the sink's 'howl.level', 0.02 to 0.003
Jeff's working-tree value 0.02 is above the rig's own documented
useful range (0.001 to 0.01). It pinned the 'howl' scene at the
clamp, and the plan's completion needs every scene below it. In
live play the value is inert: the sink's 'howl.gain' is 0, and
once the channel 2 pedal reports its target sets the level. 0.003
is that target's heel value, so every target's 'min' now equals
the sink's value. Jeff may want 0.02 back; if so, the harness
test of every scene fails on 'howl'. His own
'energy-pedal_monome' still has 0.02 and still pins 'howl'.
