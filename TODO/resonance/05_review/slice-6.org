* The two questions
** Vocabulary
*** "pedal target": two meanings
In this plan's code it always means the (parameter, min, max)
triple ('PedalTargetRig', 'StringsPedalTarget', 'pedal_targets',
'validate_pedal_targets', the README, the rig, the readme). But
the older 'settings::PedalTarget' is what a pedal's role drives,
and slice 2 added 'PedalTarget::StringParameters' beside it.
'LiveParams' and 'Settings' now hold both, as
'expression_pedals' (of 'PedalTarget') and 'pedal_targets'.
The plan foresaw the clash and kept the name; it is now in
'problems.org' under the names.
*** "targetable": one meaning
A float the engine applies without rebuilding a table, the keys
of 'TARGETABLE_PARAMETERS'. Same in 'pedal_targets.rs', the
'rig.rs' tests, the README, the rig and its readme.
*** "effective parameters": one meaning
The parameters a block renders with, targets applied:
'pedal_targets.rs', 'audio.rs', the harness. Its argument
'live_params: &StringsParams' in 'apply_pedal_targets' is the
live strings parameters, not a 'LiveParams', though the function
'reload::live_params' returns one: a small overload.
*** "parameters pedal": one meaning
A pedal of role 'string_parameters', in the code, the README and
the readme. The code also says "strings pedals" for the energy
pedal and the parameters pedal together
('validate_rig_wide_strings_pedal', 'resolve_strings_pedals',
'ResolvedStringsPedals', 'pedal_volume.rs', the readme); the
plan never coined that, but it means one thing throughout.
*** "square dance": one meaning
The existing 'dance.rs' term, the dim cell circling a sustained
note's diagonal neighbours; the key, 'Knobs', 'GridSettings',
the README and the rig use it so.
*** "floor": one meaning in this plan's work
The energy window's floor, 'energy.ex_post_energizing_floor',
everywhere the plan's diffs say it. Older code has another
floor on a pedal: the volume taper's "exponential's floor"
('pedal_volume.rs', 'rig.rs' 'curve_*' docs, the README). The
two never share a sentence, and the new one is always "the
window's floor"; worth knowing, not worth a rename.

None. No added line of the slices' Rust diffs
