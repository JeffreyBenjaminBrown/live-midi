* Scope
** In
*** A second render engine, the strings engine
Beside the sawwave engine, selected per rig by a new sink kind
'cpal_strings'. It renders the same shared voice map the grid
threads already drive, so every press, release, sustain, shift,
volume and pedal path of the surfaces runtime works unchanged.
*** A string voice
An extended Karplus-Strong loop with fractional-delay tuning, a
loss filter specified as decay time in seconds, an optional
dispersion allpass chain, one to three unison loops per note, a
deterministic pluck or strike excitation with a synthetic body
knock, and a DC blocker.
*** The junction
One shared bridge that couples every sounding loop, passive by
construction, with its heaviness as the one knob.
*** The body
A bank of parallel resonators on the summed output.
*** The energy pedal
A new expression-pedal role whose history pre-charges a string
at key-down and whose live position drives the string for a
window after key-down, through Jeff's asymmetric triangle and
exponent.
*** The listening harness
A binary that renders scripted scenes to WAV files from a rig's
string parameters, so parameters can be judged by ear without
grids.
*** The rig 'energy-pedal_monome' and its readme
*** Hot reload of every string parameter
*** The options
The howl path, the modal crossover for the top register, and
the bow and wind energy kinds.
** Out
Recorded impulse responses and convolution (synthetic body
only; Jeff chose synthetic). Positive-real bridge filters with
modes (the bridge is a scalar and a one-pole lowpass; a modal
bridge is a later plan). Reworking the old rigs beyond what the
changed types force. Velocity.

* Vocabulary
Terms coined or fixed by this plan. Code, docs and rig keys use
these words and no synonyms.
** The sawwave engine
The existing per-voice oscillator render in
'edo_surface/src/sawwave/voices.rs' ('accumulate_voices',
'BlockRenderer'). Rigs select it with a 'cpal_synth' sink.
** The strings engine
The new render in 'edo_surface/src/strings/'. Rigs select it
with a 'cpal_strings' sink.
** Voice control state
The 'VoiceState' in 'sawwave/types.rs': the control-rate facts
about a voice (frequency, envelope, the gain components) that
both engines read. The grid threads write it; neither engine
owns it. It stays 'Copy' and heap-free.
** String voice
The strings engine's per-voice audio state, keyed by 'VoiceId'.
It holds one or more loops and the exciter and drive state.
** Loop
One delay-line loop: delay line, tuning allpass, dispersion
chain, loss filter, DC blocker. A string voice with
'unison_count' 2 has two loops, each a member of the junction.
** Junction
The shared bridge. Every loop hands it the wave arriving at the
bridge and takes back the bridge velocity minus that wave.
** Bridge heaviness
The junction's 'r': the bridge impedance over one loop's. 'r'
near 0 is a light bridge (strong, self-normalizing coupling);
'r' large is a heavy bridge (weak, piano-like coupling).
** Body
The radiation filter on the summed output: a bank of parallel
second-order resonators. Not inside any loop.
** Knock
The body's own short response to being hit, convolved into the
exciter's table. Synthesized from the same body parameters.
** Exciter
What starts a note: the table read into the loops at key-down.
Two kinds, 'pluck' and 'strike'.
** Energy pedal
The expression pedal in role 'string_energy'. Rig-wide.
** Energy window
Per note: from key-down minus 'ex_ante_energizing_ms' to
key-down plus 'ex_post_energizing_ms'. The triangle is 0 at the
start, 1 at key-down, 0 at the end, linear on each side.
** Pre-charge
The energy a string holds at key-down from the pedal's history
over the ex-ante leg, computed as one weighted sum and written
into the loops as one period of a waveshape.
** Drive
The per-sample signal added into a loop by the energy source
during the ex-post leg. Its gain is 'k'.
** Growth rate
Nepers per second of amplitude growth the drive aims for; the
pedal maps to this, and 'k' is derived from it per note so the
feel is register-independent.
** Howl path
The optional active loop: sum of all loops, gain, tanh, delay,
added into every loop. Off by default.
** Crossover
The pitch above which a note is a modal voice (a few resonators)
instead of loops.
** Energy kind
Which continuous-energy model the drive uses: 'ebow', 'bow' or
'wind'.
** Harness
The 'strings_harness' binary in 'tools/strings/'.

* Agreed behavior
** The strings engine renders the shared voice map
*** Every 'VoiceState' in the map is a sounding string voice
The engine keeps a string voice for each 'VoiceState.id' it
sees. A new id spawns a string voice tuned to 'freq' and reads
the exciter table (scaled by 'pick_energy') plus the pre-charge
into its loops. An id that leaves the map drops its string
voice. A 'VoiceState' whose 'env' and 'target_env' are both 0
leaves the map exactly as it does now.
*** The output of a voice is its loops' sum times its gains
The gain coefficient is the one the sawwave engine computes:
'env * timbre.gain * fader_gain * grid_gain * new_pedal_gain *
global_pedal_gain * amplitude', with the factored pulse and the
timbre's AM ignored (the strings engine has no AM, FM or
waveform; a timbre's 'gain' still applies). The envelope is the
damper: 'note_off' ramps 'env' to 0 over 'release_secs' and the
string falls silent; a sustained (drone) voice keeps 'env' at 1
and rings on its own decay.
*** The engine retunes a loop when its voice's 'freq' moves
Glides, pedal slides, edit retunes and the crowded-pitch ratio
all move 'freq'. When a loop's target pitch differs from its
current one by more than 0.01 percent, the loop retunes (delay
length, tuning allpass, loss and dispersion coefficients).
*** Keying by id survives a sustain
'SurfaceSink::sustain_note' moves a 'VoiceState' from its cell
key to a drone key without changing 'id', so the string voice
continues. Keying by 'VoiceSource' would restart it; the engine
must key by 'VoiceId'.
*** Silent strings are reaped
A string voice whose voice has been in the map for over one
second and whose loops' energy has fallen below 1e-10 while no
window is open is dropped from the engine (not from the map; the
map's entry stays until the runtime removes it) and respawned
if the voice is struck again. This keeps a hundred silent drones
from costing a hundred delay lines.
**** TODO UPDATED [Claude] after slice 3: no reaping
A reaped string cannot ring in sympathy, and a sustained drone
is exactly the string Jeff wants other keys to excite. A loop
costs little (32 coupled loops ran at about one percent of a
core in the discussion's simulation). So a string voice lives
as long as its 'VoiceState' is in the map, silent or not, and
nothing else drops it. Slice 4 removes slice 3's reaper.
** One loop
*** Period and tuning
The loop's round trip is 'P = sample_rate / f0' samples. It is
'N' integer samples of delay plus a first-order Thiran allpass
with fractional delay 'D' in [0.5, 1.5), minus the phase delay
at 'f0' of the loss filter and of the dispersion chain, all
subtracted so the loop sounds at 'f0'. 'a = (1 - D) / (1 + D)'
and 'H(z) = (a + z^-1) / (1 + a z^-1)'. A unit test asserts the
rendered pitch within 2 cents at 55, 440, 1760 and 3000 Hz.
*** Loss
A one-pole 'H(z) = g (1 + a) / (1 + a z^-1)', 'a' in (-1, 0].
The per-round-trip gain at 'f0' is
'g = 10 ^ (-3 / (f0 * T60(f0)))' where 'T60(f0)' is the decay
time curve: log-linear in 'log2(f0)' between
'string.t60_seconds_at_55hz' and 'string.t60_seconds_at_1760hz',
extrapolated beyond. 'a' is set so that the round-trip gain at
the reference frequency 'string.high_frequency_hz' (default
4000) is 'g_hf = 10 ^ (-3 / (f0 * T60(f0) *
string.high_frequency_t60_ratio))', solved in closed form from
'|H(e^jw)|^2 = g^2 (1 + a)^2 / (1 + 2 a cos w + a^2)'. If the
reference frequency is at or above Nyquist, 'a' is 0. A unit
test asserts a rendered T60 within 10 percent at 110 and 880 Hz.
*** Dispersion
'M = string.dispersion_sections' identical first-order allpass
sections with the Rauhala-Välimäki coefficient from 'B(f0)' and
'f0', as in Faust's 'piano_dispersion_filter' (the formula and
constants are quoted in
'01_discussion/lit_strings-and-bodies.org' under "Fix 6"). 'B'
is log-linear in 'log2(f0)' between
'string.inharmonicity_b_at_55hz' and
'string.inharmonicity_b_at_1760hz'. The chain is skipped
(zero sections, zero delay) when 'M' is 0 or when 'B' is below
the perceptual threshold 'exp(2.54 ln f0 - 24.6)'. Its phase
delay at 'f0' is subtracted from the loop delay.
*** DC blocker
'y = x - x1 + 0.995 y1' inside the loop, after the loss filter.
*** Unisons
'string.unison_count' loops (1 to 3), the k-th detuned by
'(k - (count - 1) / 2) * string.unison_detune_cents', all with
the same loss. The exciter table is split equally among them.
Each is its own junction member.
** The junction
*** The formula
Per sample, with 'N' the number of loops currently in the
engine and 'r = bridge.heaviness':
'v_b = L( (2 / (N + r)) * sum_i v_i )', where 'v_i' is the wave
arriving at the bridge from loop i (its delay-line read after
tuning, dispersion and loss) and 'L' is a one-pole lowpass with
DC gain 1 and cutoff 'bridge.damping_hz'. Each loop then writes
'v_i - v_b + excitation_i + drive_i' into its delay line (the
nut's inversion folded in). With 'r > 0' and 'L' of gain at
most 1 the junction removes energy or leaves it; it never adds.
*** Stability test
A unit test excites 32 loops at pitches including unisons,
octaves and fifths, with no drive, and asserts the total stored
energy (sum of squares of all delay lines) never rises from one
block to the next after the excitation ends, for 'r' in {1, 50,
500}.
*** TODO UPDATED [Claude] after slice 3: the bridge is lossy
A real-valued bridge impedance is a resistance: the in-phase
mode of a voice's loops loses the factor '(r - N) / (r + N)'
per round trip, and a lone loop loses '1 - 2 / (N + r)'. At
'r = 50' a 110 Hz note reached -40 dB in 0.43 s against a T60
curve of 6.8 s. This is physics, not a bug: a light bridge
radiates. The T60 curve therefore means the string's decay
against a rigid bridge, and the bridge adds its own loss, which
grows with pitch (more round trips per second): at 'r = 3000'
the bridge alone gives a T60 of about 42 s at 110 Hz and 2.6 s
at 1760 Hz. The default heaviness becomes 3000 (slice 4 changes
'StringsParams::default()'), and the rig's documentation of
'bridge.heaviness' (slice 6) states this trade: smaller 'r' is
stronger sympathy and shorter notes, together. Compensating the
loss filter for the bridge is unsafe: the anti-phase modes see
the junction as unity, so a loss filter above 'g' can exceed 1
there.
*** 'r' may not be 0
Validation rejects 'bridge.heaviness <= 0'. (At 'r = 0' and 'N
= 1' the write is '-v', which halves the pitch.)
*** Per sample, inside the callback
The junction runs sample by sample. No whole-buffer delay sits
in the coupling path (the discussion records why: it diverges).
** The body
'body.mode_count' parallel second-order resonators, center
frequencies log-spaced from 'body.lowest_hz' to
'body.highest_hz' with a deterministic per-mode jitter from
'body.seed', each of bandwidth 'f_k / body.q', with gains
falling as '1 / sqrt(f_k / lowest_hz)'. Output is
'(1 - body.mix) * dry + body.mix * wet / norm' where 'norm'
makes a white-noise input's RMS equal in and out. 'mix' 0 is
exactly the dry sum. The same bank, impulse-excited and
multiplied by 'exp(-t / (knock_ms / 6.9))', is the knock table.
** The exciter
*** Pluck
White noise from a fixed seed, one period long, through a
one-pole lowpass whose cutoff is 'exciter.brightness' times
'f0' (clamped below Nyquist), then the position comb
'1 - z^-round(position * P)' with 'exciter.position' in (0,
0.5], then convolved with the knock table scaled by
'exciter.knock_mix'. Total amplitude normalized so the table's
peak is 'exciter.pick_energy'.
*** Strike
'exciter.strike_pulses' raised-cosine pulses, each of width
'exciter.hammer_contact_fraction * P' samples, spaced one pulse
width apart, through the same lowpass, comb and knock,
normalized the same way.
*** Determinism
The same parameters and pitch give the same table every time.
** The energy pedal
*** Role and plumbing
A new 'PedalRole::StringEnergy' ('role = "string_energy"'),
rig-wide (its 'monome' is ignored if given and may be left
out), at most one per rig, and only on a rig whose play grids
use a 'cpal_strings' sink (validation rejects it otherwise).
'pedal_toggle' windows may not name this role. The pedal thread
publishes the pedal's normalized position (through its taper,
so 'range_db' and the curve knobs work as for the other roles)
into one shared 'AtomicU32' of f32 bits, NaN until the first
CC. The strings engine reads it every callback and keeps its own
history ring of one position per callback, long enough for the
longest 'ex_ante_energizing_ms' (10 s).
*** The window
For a string voice born at engine time 'T' (the callback in
which its id first appears), 'tri(t)' is
'(t - (T - ex_ante)) / ex_ante' on '[T - ex_ante, T]',
'1 - (t - T) / ex_post' on '[T, T + ex_post]', and 0 elsewhere.
'ex_ante = energy.ex_ante_energizing_ms', 'ex_post =
energy.ex_post_energizing_ms'. 'pedal(t)' is the history
position at 't', taken as 0 where unreported (NaN).
'e = energy.pedal_energy_exponent' (validated > 0). The energy
factor is 'tri(t) * pedal(t) ^ e'.
*** The pre-charge
At birth the engine computes
'A0 = energy.precharge_gain * sum_j dt * tri(t_j) * pedal(t_j)^e
* exp(-(T - t_j) / tau)' over the history samples in the
ex-ante leg, with 'dt' the callback period in seconds and
'tau = T60(f0) / ln(1000)' the voice's amplitude time constant.
It then adds to each loop's delay line one period of the
pre-charge waveshape scaled by 'A0 / unison_count': for 'ebow'
a sine at 'f0' plus 0.2 of a sine at '2 f0'; for 'bow' a
sawtooth through the loop's own loss filter; for 'wind' a sine
at the partial nearest the Strouhal frequency (see the wind
kind below). With no pedal history (never reported) 'A0' is 0.
*** The drive, ebow kind
During the ex-post leg each loop adds per sample
'drive = k * A * tanh(v / A)', with 'v' the loop's own arriving
wave, 'A = energy.drive_limit', and 'k = max(0, exp(rate / f0)
- g)' where 'rate = energy.growth_rate_per_second_max * tri(t)
* pedal(t)^e' and 'g' is the loop's round-trip gain at 'f0'.
Below the natural loss 'k' is 0 and the drive does nothing.
Outside the window 'k' is 0. A test asserts that a note with a
full pedal and a 1 s ex-post leg grows and a note with the pedal
at 0 decays as without the pedal.
*** TODO UPDATED [Claude] after slice 5: the rate's heel
As written above, 'k = max(0, exp(rate / f0) - g)' with 'rate =
0' at the heel gives 'k = 1 - g', which exactly cancels the loss
and holds a heel-pedal note forever. Slice 5 maps the pedal
onto the rate from the string's own decay rate (so 'k = 0' at
the heel) up to 'growth_rate_per_second_max' at full pedal and
full triangle. 'energy.rs' derives it.
*** Only the window gates the drive
Whether a finger holds the note, whether it is sustained, and
whether it has been retriggered do not enter. A re-strike makes
a new 'VoiceState' (a new id), hence a new window.
** Hot reload
Every string parameter is in 'LiveParams' (so 'StringsParams'
is 'Copy'). The engine compares the params it last used with
the ones read each callback: a change to loss, dispersion,
bridge, body, howl or crossover parameters is applied to
sounding loops at once (filters recomputed; the body bank
rebuilt); a change to exciter or energy parameters applies to
notes born afterward. A test adopts a rig with a changed
't60_seconds_at_55hz' through 'adopt_rig' and asserts the
renderer's next block uses it.
** The harness
'cargo run -p tools --bin strings_harness -- [--rig NAME]
[--scene NAME] [--out DIR]' renders each scene to
'DIR/<scene>.wav' (default 'target/strings-harness/', mono f32,
the rig's sample rate), printing each file's path and peak.
Without '--rig' it uses 'StringsParams::default()'. Scenes:
'single_notes' (55, 110, 220, 440, 880, 1760 Hz, each 2 s with
the damper at 1.5 s), 'chord' (four notes held 4 s),
'restrike' (the chord sustained, one note struck again at 2 s),
'pedal_swell' (pedal 0 to 1 over the second before a key-down,
held through the ex-post leg), 'sympathy' (one loud note struck
while four others ring quietly). The harness drives the engine
exactly as the runtime does: by inserting and editing
'VoiceState's in a 'VoiceMap' and calling the engine's block
render, so what it renders is what the rig plays.
** The rig 'energy-pedal_monome'
Derived from 'volpedal-newnotes-and-global_granular-sustain':
the same tuning (41-EDO, 110 Hz, x-step 8, y-step 1), two
unpinned 16x16 grids of which either may be absent, each with
an 'edo_note_grid' with 'sustain_handle = "below"' and
'flash_sustained', an 'edo_shift_pad' at [13, 0, 15, 1], and
the three 'accrete_control' cells at (6, 15), (7, 15), (8, 15)
with 'needs_holding' initially on. No waveform selector, no
volume strip, no pedal toggles. One 'cpal_strings' sink. One
'[[expression_pedals]]' entry, channel 1, role 'string_energy'.
Its readme follows the volpedal readme's shape.
** The options
*** Howl path
'howl.gain' (0 = off), 'howl.level', 'howl.delay_ms' in [0.5,
20]. Per sample: 'h = howl.level * tanh(howl.gain * sum_i v_i)'
is delayed 'howl.delay_ms' and added into every loop's write.
Bounded for any gain because tanh is.
*** Modal crossover
A note whose 'f0' is above 'modal.crossover_hz' is rendered by
'modal.mode_count' second-order resonators at
'k f0 sqrt(1 + B k^2)' with per-mode round-trip-equivalent
decay from the same T60 curve and ratio, excited by the same
table, instead of loops. It is a junction member whose arriving
wave is its output and which adds '-v_b' to its input. The
crossover defaults to 2000 Hz; 0 disables it.
*** Bow kind
'energy.kind = "bow"': the drive is the STK friction table,
'bow = (|(v_b - v) * slope| + 0.75)^-4' clamped to [0.0025,
0.245] with 'v_b = energy.bow_velocity * tri(t) * pedal(t)^e',
added as '(v_b - v) * bow', with 'slope = energy.bow_force' in
[1, 10]. Expect non-starts at extreme settings; the readme says
so.
*** Wind kind
'energy.kind = "wind"': the drive is white noise through a
second-order bandpass at the Strouhal frequency
'f_s = energy.wind_speed_hz_max * pedal(t)^e' with Q
'energy.wind_q', scaled by 'tri(t) * energy.wind_gain'. The
partial nearest 'f_s' blooms.
** What stays as it is
*** Every 'cpal_synth' rig renders bit-identically
The control-stepping refactor must not change the sawwave
output. The agent proves it by rendering a scripted voice map
before and after the refactor and diffing the buffers (a
one-time check, not a workspace member).
*** The grid threads do not know which engine renders
No surfaces-runtime module outside 'audio.rs', 'settings.rs',
'reload.rs' and 'pedal_volume.rs' changes for the engine.
*** Default behaviors Jeff sees
Sustain via the handle, the three sustain buttons' semantics,
the shift pad, 'r' reload, the red report for missing gear, and
the pedal bridge requirement all behave as the volpedal readme
describes.

* Pitfalls the slices must respect
Facts about the existing code, with where they live.
** The sawwave render reaps voices inside 'accumulate_voices'
'voices.retain(...)' in 'sawwave/voices.rs' drops a voice when
'env == 0 && target_env == 0'. The strings engine must do the
same reaping (the runtime relies on the map shrinking) and must
drop the matching string voice.
** 'VoiceState' is 'Copy' and the grid threads clone it
Every grid thread snapshots and edits 'VoiceState's under the
map lock ('surfaces_runtime/synth.rs', some 30 free functions).
Adding a heap field would break 'Copy'; adding any field means
touching every constructor ('note_on_with_phase_and_gain',
'spawn_accretion_voice', the looper's sink, the tests). Do not
add fields. The engine's per-voice state lives in the engine.
** The audio callback owns the renderer
'surfaces_runtime/audio.rs' moves a 'BlockRenderer' into the
cpal closure and reads 'live.params' every callback. The
strings engine's renderer is owned the same way; it is never
shared with a grid thread. 'start_null' (headless runs) builds
no renderer at all.
** 'resolve_settings' insists on 'cpal_synth'
'surfaces_runtime/settings.rs' lines 254-280 find the first
'edo_note_grid', look up its sink and destructure
'SinkRig::CpalSynth', erroring otherwise. This is the one place
the strings sink is admitted. The sawwave-only fields it fills
('oversample', 'sustain_level', 'decay_secs', the distortion
knobs, 'crowded_pitch_planck_deviation',
'retrigger_tail_detune_cents', 'attack') take these values for
a strings sink: oversample 1, sustain_level 1.0, decay_secs 0
(the pluck envelope off; 'pluck_envelope' returns (peak, 1.0)
for either), attack 0.001, distortion off with makeup 1.0 and
auto off, crowded deviation 0, retrigger detune 0.
** 'LiveParams' is 'Copy' and read every callback
'surfaces_runtime/reload.rs': 'live_params' builds it from
'Settings'; 'adopt_rig' replaces it and bumps 'generation';
'refresh_live' copies the grid-thread subset. 'StringsParams'
goes in as 'Option<StringsParams>' ('None' for sawwave rigs)
and must be 'Copy', so it holds no 'Vec'; the body's mode table
is derived in the renderer.
** The pedal thread dispatches on 'PedalTarget'
'surfaces_runtime/pedal_volume.rs', 'expression_pedal_loop':
the 'match target' handles 'Grid' and, in one arm, 'NewNote'
and 'Global', which 'continue' before the grid-volume code. A
'StringEnergy' arm goes beside them. 'PedalTarget' is in
'settings.rs' (line 227) and resolved from 'PedalRole' at line
590. 'LiveParams.expression_pedals' is read fresh each poll, so
the role is hot-reloadable like the others.
** Pedal validation lives in two functions
'rig.rs' 'validate_expression_pedals' (line 2109) and
'validate_pedal_toggles' (line 2236). The first matches on
'PedalRole' exhaustively (a new variant must be handled); the
second rejects 'GridVolume' for toggles and must also reject
'StringEnergy'. 'PedalRole' has '#[default] GridVolume' and
'rename_all = "snake_case"'.
** 'SinkRig' is a tagged enum with 'deny_unknown_fields'
'rig.rs' line 633: '#[serde(tag = "kind", rename_all =
"snake_case", deny_unknown_fields)]'. A nested struct field
(e.g. 'string: StringRig') inside the 'CpalStrings' variant
gives '[[sinks]].string.t60_seconds_at_55hz' in TOML, which in
the org format is a 'TABLE string' under the 'ELEM sinks'.
Grouping headlines between them are transparent
('rig_org.rs' line 13: "its descendants attach to the nearest
enclosing TABLE/ELEM"). Nested structs need their own
'deny_unknown_fields' so a misspelled key is loud.
** Every rig in 'rigs/' is loaded by a test
'rig.rs' 'loads_default_rigs' (line 3399) parses every
non-readme '.org' in 'rigs/'. The new rig must parse and
validate from the moment it is added.
** Mock-grid tests serialize and rewrite ports
'surfaces_runtime/tests.rs': 'MOCK_LOCK' serializes them;
'volpedal_rig_runs_against_one_and_two_mock_grids' (line 2474)
is the model: it loads the rig, rewrites the two
'listen_port's to free ones, resolves settings, and drives the
mock grids. The new rig's test copies that shape and reads
every cell it presses from the resolved settings, never from
literals ('README_AI.org', "Tests must read the rig").
** The dispatch binary does not care about the sink kind
'buried/midi_pulse/midi_pulse.rs' 'is_surfaces_rig' (line 126)

** DONE Slice 3: junction, body, renderer, harness
*** Corrections carried from slice 2
**** Stream the exciter; do not fold it into the delay line
Slice 2's 'Loop::add_table' adds a table into the ring at once,
wrapping a table longer than the line onto itself, which raises
peaks at high pitch. The string voice will instead hold its
excitation table and a read position and add one sample of it
to each loop write ('write(v_i - v_b + table[pos] /
unison_count + drive_i)'), so a table of any length streams in
over time, as STK's 'Guitar' streams its commuted body file.
'add_table' stays for the pre-charge, which is exactly one
period.
**** The knock is a parallel table, not a per-note convolution
The convolution of the dry table with the knock costs 'P * K'
multiply-adds at note-on (about 2.5 million at 55 Hz), too
much inside a 128-frame callback. 'exciter.rs' will split into
'dry_table(params, f0, sample_rate)' (noise or pulses, lowpass,
comb, normalized to peak 'pick_energy') and
'knock_table(params, body, sample_rate)' (the body impulse
response times the exponential window, through the same
one-pole lowpass at a fixed cutoff of 'brightness * 110' Hz,
normalized to unit energy, scaled by 'knock_mix * pick_energy').
The knock table is computed once per parameter change by the
renderer, never per note; each voice streams both tables in
parallel from its birth. The exciter tests adapt: determinism,
dry peak equals 'pick_energy', knock energy equals
'(knock_mix * pick_energy)^2'.
**** Slice 2's loop facts the renderer relies on
The DC blocker's pole follows 'f0 / 200'; the tuning allpass is
exact at 'f0'; the loss meets 'g' at 'f0'; 'Loop::energy' sums
only the in-flight samples; 'first_order.rs' holds the shared
first-order filter the junction's lowpass should reuse;
'noise.rs' holds the seeded LCG.

** DONE Slice 6: the rig, its readme, the mock run, user docs

**** The drive limit rises with the pedal
Slice 5 found that at 'drive_limit' 0.4 the tanh flattens the
bloom about 5 dB up within 0.3 s. The limit will scale with the
pedal: 'A = drive_limit * pedal(t)^e' (so a light pedal blooms
