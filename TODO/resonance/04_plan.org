* Assignment and authority
This plan is the executable form of what Jeff asked for on
2026-10-31 after playing 'energy-pedal_monome': that an
expression pedal may be assigned not to one role but to any
number of (parameter, min, max) triples, each its own org
headline under which he can write what he likes; that a second
pedal should push the strings into feedback, "like a guitar
close to an amp"; and that this lands in a new rig beside the
one he is tuning, which this plan does not touch. Nothing here
is open for discussion. The run follows 'coding-advice/
orchestration.org': one Opus 5.5 agent per slice at high
effort, the orchestrator (Claude Fable) reviewing each slice
and keeping the record in '05_review.org'. The target state:
** A pedal may carry targets
Under a pedal's 'ELEM expression_pedals', any number of
'ELEM targets' headlines, each with 'PARAM parameter',
'PARAM min' and 'PARAM max', and any prose. The rig format
already nests them this way ('rig_org.rs'); no syntax changes.
** The channel 1 pedal is the energy pedal and sets the pluck
Its role stays 'string_energy'; its one target is
'exciter.pick_energy', so the heel is a soft pluck and the toe a
hard one, while its history and window do what they do now.
** The channel 2 pedal brings on the squeal
A pedal of the new role 'string_parameters' with targets on the
howl path and the bridge: at the heel the rig sounds exactly as
without it, at the toe every note sustains and brightens.
** Everything hot-reloads
A target added, removed or re-ranged in the rig applies at the
next 'r' Enter, through the pedal's current position.
** No square dance
When a note is sustained through its handle, the four cells
diagonal to it start flashing in a circle (the "square dance",

rig: a grid gains a key to turn it off.
** The rig 'two-pedals_monome'
A copy of Jeff's 'energy-pedal_monome' with its pedal section

* Scope
** In
*** Pedal targets
A rig-level notion: an expression pedal carries a list of
targets, each naming one float parameter of the strings engine
and the values it takes at the heel and at the toe. The engine
renders with the rig's parameters overridden by every target
whose pedal has reported.
*** The role 'string_parameters'
A pedal whose only job is its targets.
*** Per-channel pedal positions
The pedal thread publishes every pedal's raw position, one
atomic per MPC-20 channel; the audio callback reads what the
live parameters say each one means. This replaces the single
'string_energy_position' word.
*** The knock scaled at birth
So that 'exciter.pick_energy' and 'exciter.knock_mix' can move
at pedal rate without rebuilding the knock table.
*** 'body.mix' live
So that it can be a target without rebuilding the bank.
*** The energy window's floor
A new energy parameter: the level the ex-post leg ramps down
to, instead of always 0.
*** The harness renders both pedals and the rig's targets
*** The grid key 'square_dance'
*** The rig 'two-pedals_monome', its readme, user docs
** Out
Targets on integer or enum parameters; targets on parameters
whose change rebuilds a table (listed under "Agreed behavior");
per-target tapers or exponents (linear in the raw position
only; Jeff asked for triples); pedal toggles for the new role;
any change to 'rigs/energy-pedal_monome.org' or its readme;
the sawwave engine's parameters as targets; everything in
'TODO/problems.org' except what the slices touch in passing.

* Vocabulary
Terms coined or fixed by this plan. Code, docs and rig keys use
these words and no synonyms. The words of '02_plan.org' (string
voice, loop, junction, body, knock, exciter, energy pedal,
energy window, howl path, harness) keep their meanings.
** Pedal target
One (parameter, min, max) triple on an expression pedal: the
rig's 'ELEM targets' under an 'ELEM expression_pedals'. In
code, 'PedalTargetRig' in the rig and 'StringsPedalTarget'
resolved. Not to be confused with the existing
'settings::PedalTarget', which names what a pedal's role drives
(a grid, every grid, the energy); this plan renames nothing
there.
** Target parameter
The strings parameter a pedal target names, spelled as the
sink's validation messages already spell it: the table and the
key, 'howl.gain', 'exciter.pick_energy', 'bridge.heaviness'.
** Targetable
A strings parameter that may be a target parameter: a float
whose change the engine applies without rebuilding a table.
The one list is 'strings/pedal_targets.rs'.
** Pedal position
A pedal's raw normalized position in [0, 1], 0 at the heel, 1
at the toe, as 'expression_pedals::Pedal::norm' reports it. NaN
means the pedal has never reported.
** Effective parameters
The 'StringsParams' the engine renders a block with: the live
parameters with every target of every reported pedal applied.
** The parameters pedal
A pedal of role 'string_parameters'. Rig-wide, like the energy
pedal.
** Square dance
The codebase's existing name ('dance.rs') for the single dim
cell that circles a sustained note's four diagonal neighbours.
The diamond dance is its twin for an edited note, at the four
orthogonal neighbours; this plan leaves the diamond alone.

* Agreed behavior
** The rig
*** The schema
'ExpressionPedalRig' gains 'targets: Vec<PedalTargetRig>',
serde default empty. 'PedalTargetRig { parameter: String, min:
f32, max: f32 }' with 'deny_unknown_fields'. In org:
  #+begin_example
  *** ELEM expression_pedals
  **** PARAM channel = 2
  **** PARAM role = "string_parameters"
  **** ELEM targets
  ***** PARAM parameter = "howl.gain"
  ***** PARAM min = 0.0
  ***** PARAM max = 20.0
  Any prose here is ignored by the loader.
  **** ELEM targets
  ***** PARAM parameter = "howl.level"
  ***** PARAM min = 0.003
  ***** PARAM max = 0.006
  #+end_example
'rig_org.rs' already turns a nested 'ELEM targets' into the
pedal's 'targets' array (its frame stack closes by org depth,
and grouping headlines are transparent); a test in slice 1
asserts it for this exact shape, including a grouping headline
between the pedal and its targets and prose under a target.
*** The role
'PedalRole::StringParameters' ('role = "string_parameters"').
Rig-wide like 'string_energy': it names no 'monome', takes none
of the taper knobs, and needs a 'cpal_strings' sink under the
play grids. Unlike the energy pedal, a rig may have one per
channel. It must have at least one target; a parameters pedal
without targets is refused ("a string_parameters pedal with no
targets does nothing").
*** Which pedals may carry targets
Any pedal in role 'string_energy' or 'string_parameters'. A
pedal in a volume role with targets is refused, naming the two
roles that take them: the volume roles' positions pass through
a taper, and a target wants the raw position.
*** Validation of a target
Each is checked when the rig loads, so a typo fails at 'r' with
the rig's old parameters still playing, never mid-performance:
**** The parameter must be targetable
Otherwise the error names the parameter and lists every
targetable one.
**** The same parameter at most once across the rig's pedals
Two pedals, or two targets of one pedal, on one parameter are
refused: which would win is a question nobody should answer.
**** min and max must each be legal values
The whole 'StringsParams' with that parameter set to 'min' must
pass 'StringsParams::validate', and so must it with 'max'. Every
validated range is an interval and the one coupled constraint
(the strike's span in periods) is monotone in its float, so
every position between is legal too. 'min' above 'max' is
allowed and inverts the pedal. 'min' equal to 'max' is allowed
and pins the parameter (a way to try a value without editing
the sink).
** The runtime
*** Per-channel positions
'mod.rs' makes 'pedal_positions: Arc<[AtomicU32; NUM_PEDALS]>',
each NaN bits. The pedal thread stores pedal 'i''s 'norm' into
slot 'i' whenever 'updates > 0', for every pedal in the reader
whatever its role or whether the rig binds it; a slot never
returns to NaN once set (a reload cannot un-report a pedal, and
what the position means is the live parameters' business). The
single 'string_energy_position' and its NaN-on-unbound rule go.
*** What the live parameters carry
'LiveParams' gains 'pedal_targets: [[Option<StringsPedalTarget>;
MAX_PEDAL_TARGETS]; NUM_PEDALS]' and 'string_energy_pedal:
Option<usize>' (the channel index of the energy pedal, 'None'
without one). 'MAX_PEDAL_TARGETS' is 16, enough for every
targetable parameter at once on one pedal; validation refuses
more per pedal. 'LiveParams' stays 'Copy'.
*** 'StringsPedalTarget'
'Copy': the parameter's key as '&'static str' (for messages and
tests), its setter 'fn(&mut StringsParams, f32)', 'min', 'max'.
Resolved from a 'PedalTargetRig' by 'strings/pedal_targets.rs'.
*** Effective parameters each callback
In 'audio.rs', after reading 'p.strings', the callback reads
each slot of 'pedal_positions' and applies every target of the
pedals that have reported: 'value = min + (max - min) *
position', 'setter(&mut params, value)'. A pedal that has never
reported leaves its targets' parameters at the rig's values.
The energy pedal's position is slot 'string_energy_pedal', NaN
when 'None'. The function that does this,
'strings::pedal_targets::apply_pedal_targets(params, targets,
positions) -> StringsParams', is pure, so the harness and the
tests call the same one.
*** The engine adopts the effective parameters as it does now
'StringsRenderer::render_block' already compares the parameters
it is handed with the last ones and adopts a change
('adopt_params'). A moving pedal therefore retunes every voice
each block when it targets a 'string' key, reconfigures the
junction for a 'bridge' key and the howl for a 'howl' key, and
reaches notes born afterward for an 'exciter' or 'energy' key.
No new path into the engine.
*** Hot reload
'reload::live_params' resolves the targets and the energy
channel from 'Settings'; 'adopt_rig' replaces them with the
rest. A reload that removes a pedal's targets leaves the rig's
values in force from the next callback; one that adds targets
applies them through the position already in the slot.
** The engine
*** The knock is scaled at birth
'exciter::knock_table' returns the body's windowed, lowpassed
impulse response normalized to unit energy (sum of squares 1),
and depends on 'exciter.brightness', 'exciter.knock_ms' and the
body alone. 'ExcitationStream' gains a 'knock_scale: f32' set at
birth to 'knock_mix * pick_energy' and multiplies the knock
sample by it. 'adopt_params' rebuilds the knock only when those
three inputs change (compare the fields, not the struct). The
rendered attack is unchanged to within f32 rounding; a test
renders a struck note before and after the change at the
defaults and asserts the peak and the energy within 1e-4
relative. 'knock_mix' 0 is still an empty knock.
*** 'body.mix' is live
'BodyBank' gains 'set_mix'; 'adopt_params' calls it when only
'mix' changed and rebuilds the bank only when 'mode_count',
'lowest_hz', 'highest_hz', 'q' or 'seed' changed. The knock
rebuild follows the same five fields.
*** The energy window's floor
'EnergyParams' gains 'ex_post_energizing_floor: f32', unitless
in [0, 1], default 0, validated in that range, rig key
'energy.ex_post_energizing_floor'. 'energy::tri' on the ex-post
leg becomes 'floor + (1 - floor) * (1 - t / ex_post)' for 't' in
'(0, ex_post]' and 'floor' after it, so that with a floor above
0 the window never closes on the ex-post side: the drive goes on
at 'floor * pedal ^ e' for as long as the note sounds and the
pedal is down. 'tri' takes the floor as an argument; the ex-ante
leg and the value 1 at birth are unchanged, so the pre-charge
('precharge_amplitude') is unaffected. 'ex_post_energy_factor'
keeps returning 0 when the triangle is 0 (floor 0 after the leg,
as today). At the default of 0 every existing test and render
is unchanged. The ebow's rate at factor 'f' is already 'f *
growth_rate_per_second_max - (1 - f) * natural_decay_rate', so a
floor above 'decay / (growth_max + decay)' makes a held-pedal
note grow to its limit instead of settling; the rig's text for
the key says so. A voice keeps the energy parameters of its
birth, so a changed floor reaches notes struck afterward.
*** The targetable parameters
Exactly these, as 'strings/pedal_targets.rs' lists them; the list
is the one source and the rig README quotes it:
'string.t60_seconds_at_55hz', 'string.t60_seconds_at_1760hz',
'string.high_frequency_hz', 'string.high_frequency_t60_ratio',
'string.unison_detune_cents', 'string.inharmonicity_b_at_55hz',
'string.inharmonicity_b_at_1760hz', 'exciter.pick_energy',
'exciter.position', 'exciter.hammer_contact_fraction',
'exciter.knock_mix', 'energy.ex_ante_energizing_ms',
'energy.ex_post_energizing_ms',
'energy.ex_post_energizing_floor',
'energy.pedal_energy_exponent',
'energy.growth_rate_per_second_max', 'energy.drive_limit',
'energy.precharge_gain', 'energy.bow_velocity',
'energy.bow_slipperiness', 'energy.wind_speed_hz_max',
'energy.wind_q', 'energy.wind_gain', 'bridge.heaviness',
'bridge.damping_hz', 'body.mix', 'howl.gain', 'howl.level',
'howl.delay_ms', 'modal.crossover_hz'. Not targetable, and
refused by name: every integer and enum key, and
'exciter.brightness', 'exciter.knock_ms', 'body.lowest_hz',
'body.highest_hz', 'body.q' (each rebuilds a table; the body's
rebuild also measures its normalization on a second of noise). A
test asserts that every float field of 'StringsParams' is either
in the list or in the refused list, by setting each through the
list and comparing with a hand-set struct, so a field added later
must be placed.
** The square dance is a grid knob
'edo_note_grid' gains 'square_dance: bool', serde default
true, beside 'flash_sustained'. False means this grid never
draws the square dance for its sustained pitches (chord voices
included, since they share the loop in 'mod.rs'). The diamond
dance, the off-screen indicator and 'flash_sustained' are not
affected. Every existing rig behaves as before (the default is
the current behavior). The new rig sets it false on both grids.
** The harness
'--pedal TRACE' keeps its meaning (the channel 1 pedal; the
energy pedal when the rig binds one there) and '--pedal2 TRACE'
is the channel 2 pedal. The harness resolves the rig's pedal
targets and energy channel the way 'reload::live_params' does
(a shared function in 'surfaces_runtime' or in 'strings', the
agent's choice, as long as there is one) and applies them per
block through 'apply_pedal_targets'. Without '--rig' there are
no targets. A new scene 'squeal': the 'chord' scene's four
notes struck and held, the channel 2 pedal rising from 0 at 1 s
to 1 at 3 s and held to 6 s; rendered under 'two-pedals_monome'
it must grow after the pedal rises and stay below the clamp.

* Pitfalls the slices must respect
Facts about the existing code, with where they live. Read from
the code on 2026-10-31 at commit 7e11bc9.
** 'LiveParams' is 'Copy' and read under a mutex every callback
'surfaces_runtime/reload.rs' line 114 holds 'strings:
Option<StringsParams>'; 'settings.rs' line 113 holds
'expression_pedals: [Option<(PedalTarget, PedalVolumeCurve)>;
NUM_PEDALS]', a fixed array because the struct is 'Copy'. The
targets go in as fixed arrays too. 'reload::live_params' copies
'Settings' field by field (nineteen fields; 'TODO/problems.org'
notes it): a new live field is added in 'Settings', 'LiveParams'
and 'live_params'.
** Three readers of the pedal map, two writers of the position
The pedal map 'LiveParams.expression_pedals' is read by
'pedal_volume::expression_pedal_loop' (line 194, each poll) and
by nothing else; 'string_energy_position' is written there
(lines 198-212) and read in 'audio.rs' line 103 only; 'mod.rs'
line 297 creates it and passes it to both 'audio::start' (line
45 of 'audio.rs') and the pedal thread (line 526 of 'mod.rs').
'reload.rs' tests (line 403) assert the energy pedal resolves
to 'PedalTarget::StringEnergy'. Replacing the word touches all
five places and nothing else; grep 'string_energy_position'
before and after.
** The pedal thread polls; it does not get callbacks
'expression_pedal_loop' loops on 'reader.pedals()' (a copy of
both pedals' state) with a sleep, and 'p.updates == 0' is the
never-reported test. Publishing positions is one store per
pedal per poll, cheap enough to do unconditionally.
** 'resolve_settings' maps roles to targets with 'filter_map'
'settings.rs' lines 601-618: a pedal whose monome does not
resolve is silently dropped ('some_grid?'). A parameters pedal
has no monome and must not be dropped; give it its own
'PedalTarget' variant ('StringParameters') so the volume paths'
match stays exhaustive and skips it as they skip 'StringEnergy'.
** Pedal validation lives in two functions
'rig.rs' 'validate_expression_pedals' (line 2212) walks the
pedals, checks channels and monomes, and 'continue's for the
energy role after 'validate_string_energy_pedal' (line 2338),
which also finds the play grids' sink. The parameters role
takes the same branch with the same helper generalized (the
"at most one" rule applies to the energy role only). The
targets' checks go in a sibling 'validate_pedal_targets(rig)'
run from 'validate_rig' (line 1932), since the "same parameter
at most once" rule spans pedals. 'validate_pedal_toggles' (line
2396) already refuses the energy role in a toggle; refuse the
new role beside it.
** 'ExpressionPedalRig' has 'deny_unknown_fields'
'rig.rs' line 149. A 'targets' field must be declared there or
every rig with a target fails to parse. 'PedalRole' derives
'Default' as 'GridVolume' (line 187); the three rigs that omit
'role' rely on it, so the default stays.
** 'adopt_params' compares whole tables
'strings/renderer.rs' lines 176-197: 'params.body !=
previous.body' rebuilds the bank and the knock, 'params.exciter
!= previous.exciter' rebuilds the knock. 'BodyBank::new' (body.rs
line 78) measures its normalization on seconds of white noise:
far too costly per block. The field-wise comparisons under "The
engine" replace these two.
** The knock table bakes in 'pick_energy' and 'knock_mix'
'strings/exciter.rs' line 84: 'scale = knock_mix * pick_energy /
energy.sqrt()'. 'ExcitationStream::next_sample' (voice.rs line
435) adds the knock sample unscaled. 'StringVoice::new' and
'ModalVoice::new' both take the knock 'Arc'; both get the scale.
The pre-charge adds into the dry table only
('add_into_dry_table', line 454) and is unaffected.
** The dry table is built per note at birth
'renderer.rs' line 230: 'dry_table(&self.params.exciter,
voice.freq, ...)' in 'spawn_struck'. So 'pick_energy',
'position' and 'hammer_contact_fraction' already take effect at
the next note with no rebuild; only the knock stood in the way.
** 'retune' keeps the wave in flight
'loop_dsp.rs' line 129: a retune recomputes coefficients and
keeps the delay line and filter states; only a dispersion
section that joins the chain starts from rest. So a pedal on a
'string' key retunes every voice every block it moves without
a click. 'ModalVoice::retune' (modal.rs) likewise. 'Junction::
reconfigure' (junction.rs) keeps the lowpass state; 'HowlPath::
reconfigure' (howl.rs) keeps the ring unless the path was off,
and its ring is sized for 'MAX_HOWL_DELAY_MS', so 'delay_ms'
moves without allocating.
** The energy parameters are a voice's at birth
'renderer.rs' comment above 'adopt_params': "a voice keeps the
energy parameters of its birth". A target on an 'energy' key
therefore shapes the next note, not the sounding one. The rig
README says so in the targets paragraph.
** The harness builds its own render loop
'tools/strings/strings_harness.rs' lines 460-490 render blocks
with one pedal trace; 'rendering_from' (line 296) loads the rig
and takes the first strings sink's params. The targets and the
second pedal go into 'Rendering' and the loop; the harness
cannot see 'surfaces_runtime' private items ('pub(super)'), so
whatever it shares with 'reload::live_params' must be 'pub' in
'edo_surface'.
** Every rig in 'rigs/' is loaded by a test
'rig.rs' line 3563 'loads_default_rigs' loads every '.org' and
'.toml' there except readmes. The new rig is swept the moment it
exists; it must validate. 'surfaces_runtime/tests.rs' line 2607
runs 'energy-pedal_monome' through the mock grids; the new rig
gets the same run (copy the test's shape, not its body, if the
cells are the same; one helper parameterized by rig name is

** Mock-grid tests serialize
'tests.rs' holds 'MOCK_LOCK'; a mock-run test takes it. Two
full 'cargo test' runs at once collide on ports; run the suites
one at a time.
** The square dance is gated by 'Overlays::compact()' alone
'surfaces_runtime/mod.rs' line 1165: 'if !rt.overlays.compact()'
around the loop that inserts 'dance::diagonal_cell' for every
sustained and chord pitch. 'compact()' ('settings.rs' line 118)
is true for grids with a 'volume_delta', 'momentary_chord_block'
or 'compact_loop_block'; the new rig has none, so today nothing
can turn the dance off there. A grid knob reaches that line the
way 'flash_sustained' does: 'MonomeWindowRig::EdoNoteGrid'
('rig.rs' line 868), 'GridSettings' ('settings.rs' lines 143 and
550-578, where the grid's fields are pulled from the rig),
'GridThread''s knobs ('mod.rs' lines 641 and 854), the gate. The
'rigs/README.org' documents 'flash_sustained' at lines 370, 428
and 448; the new key goes beside it in all three places.
** 'rig_org.rs' closes frames by depth
Lines 69-92: a headline closes every open frame not shallower
than it, then opens its own if it is a TABLE or ELEM. An 'ELEM
targets' under a grouping headline under the pedal still
attaches to the pedal, since grouping headlines open no frame.
A test must pin this for the targets shape so a later change to
the reader cannot silently flatten them.
** Formatting and naming rules
'coding-advice/formatting.org' (types on every 'let', signature
