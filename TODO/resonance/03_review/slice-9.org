* Question (a): the terms this plan's code coined or reused
** Terms used as the vocabulary defines them
The sawwave engine, the strings engine ('strings/',
'cpal_strings'), voice control state ('VoiceState', with
'step_voice_controls' and 'VoiceStep' coined by slice 1 for its
stepping), body ('BodyBank', 'BodyModes', 'body' table), knock
('knock_table', 'knock_mix', 'knock_ms'), exciter ('exciter.rs',
'ExciterParams', 'ExciterKind', the 'exciter' table; the tables
it builds are "excitation", 'ExcitationStream', one meaning),
energy window ('tri', 'ex_ante_energizing_ms',
'ex_post_energizing_ms'), pre-charge ('precharge_amplitude',
'precharge_waveshape', 'precharge_gain'), drive ('drive',
'DriveState', 'drive_limit'), growth rate
('growth_rate_per_second_max'), howl path ('HowlPath', 'howl'
table), crossover ('crossover_hz'), energy kind ('EnergyKind':
ebow, bow, wind), harness ('strings_harness'), bridge heaviness
('bridge.heaviness'). Each is used with one meaning everywhere I
read.
** "String voice" drifted
The vocabulary's string voice is the engine's per-voice state.
In the code 'StringVoice' is the loops kind only; slice 7 added
'ModalVoice' and 'VoiceKind' and kept the renderer's map name
'string_voices' (and local 'string_voice' variables) for
'VoiceKind's. 'StringsRenderer::string_voice(id)' returns only a
loops voice, 'voice_kind(id)' either. Not renamed (no renames).
** "Loop" collides with an existing word
The vocabulary coins 'Loop' for a delay-line loop. The codebase
already says "loop" for a recorded musical loop: compact loops,
'VoiceSource::SurfaceLoop', 'end_loop_voices', the looper,
'TimbreTarget::Loop'. The strings tests' "a loop voice" (a voice
of loops) means, in 'synth.rs', a voice that a compact loop
plays. The plan did not mark the coinage against the old word.
** "Junction" and "bridge" are two words for one thing
The type is 'Junction'; the rig table is 'bridge',
'bridge_velocity' its output, 'damping_hz' its lowpass. The plan
defines the junction as "the shared bridge", so this is the
plan's own pair, not drift; Jeff meets "bridge" in the rig and
"junction" in the code.
** "Member" and "arriving wave" are coined and consistent
"Junction member" (a loop, or one modal voice) is not in the
vocabulary; it entered with slice 7 ('member_count') and has one
meaning. "Arriving wave" ('read_arriving',
'push_arriving_waves') is the vocabulary's "wave arriving at the
bridge", one meaning; for a modal voice it is the bank's output.
** "Energy" has three meanings
The energy pedal's ('EnergyParams', 'energy_factor', the 'energy'
table), a loop's stored energy ('Loop::energy',
'VoiceKind::energy': a sum of squares), and 'pick_energy', which
is a peak amplitude (the discussion's word, kept). "Drive" is
always the per-sample signal; "energy" never names the drive, so
the plan's "drive" versus "energy" holds.
** "Kind" names four enums
'ExciterKind' and 'EnergyKind' are rig choices ('kind' keys).
'VoiceKind' is not a rig key: the crossover picks it. The sink's
'kind' (serde tag, 'SinkRig::kind_name') is the old meaning. The
four do not clash in use, but "voice kind" reads like a setting.
** 'StringsParams' and 'StringParams' differ by one letter
All parameters, against the 'string' table's.
** "Power" is the pedal's exponent
'energy::pedal_power' is 'pedal ^ pedal_energy_exponent'; the
tests say "heard power" for a mean square.
** "Dry" names two things
'exciter::dry_table' (a note's own table, against the knock) and
the body's dry sum (against its wet bank).
** 'wind_speed_hz_max' is a frequency
The Strouhal frequency at full pedal, in Hz, named for the wind
speed it stands for.
** 'struck_voice_state' serves a pluck too
"Struck" is also 'ExciterKind::Strike'.
** 'bow_slipperiness' replaced a misleading word
Renamed in this slice; "force" survives only in prose about the
player's bow force, which is the physical quantity.
** Synonyms noticed in the old code
"Drone" and "sustained voice" ('SurfaceDrone', 'sustain_key',
'end_drones_at', 'cut_sustained') name one thing in
'synth.rs'.
