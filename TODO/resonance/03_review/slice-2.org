* Deviations from the plan's "how", with reasons
** The DC blocker's pole follows f0, and it is normalized
The plan's 'y = x - x1 + 0.995 y1' cuts off near 38 Hz at 48
kHz. Inside a loop it removes 18 percent of the amplitude per
round trip at 55 Hz and 5 percent at 110 Hz (designed loss: 1.2
and 0.9 percent), so no T60 test could pass and no loss design
can compensate (it would need gain above 1 at the second
partial). It also gains 2/1.995 at Nyquist, which a loop with a
flat loss filter amplifies forever. Now 'y = k (x - x1) + R y1',
'k = (1 + R) / 2', cutoff 'f0 / 200'
('DC_BLOCKER_CUTOFF_BELOW_F0'). Its loss and phase lead at f0
are folded into the loss design and the tuning. The cost: the
upper partials sit about 1.4 cents flat of exact harmonics (the
lead shrinks with frequency). 50 gave 5.5 cents; 500 would give
0.55 but leaves in-loop DC longer. Jeff may want to pick.
** The tuning allpass is exact at f0, not plain Thiran
'a = sin((1 - d) w / 2) / sin((1 + d) w / 2)' has phase delay
exactly 'd' at 'w0'; it tends to '(1 - d) / (1 + d)' at low
pitch. Plain Thiran is off by up to 2.4 cents at 3000 Hz (period
16 samples), past the 2-cent test. Near Nyquist, where no
stable first-order allpass has the asked delay, it falls back to
Thiran ('LARGEST_EXACT_ALLPASS_COEFFICIENT' 0.5); found by a
sweep that blew up at 20 kHz.
** The loss meets g at f0, not at DC
The plan's 'g (1 + a) / (1 + a z^-1)' has DC gain g. Its gain at
f0 is lower, which at 880 Hz made the decay about 11 percent too
fast. The design solves for the loop's gain (loss times DC
blocker) to be 'g' at f0 and 'g_hf' at the reference, the same
closed-form quadratic with 'cos w0' in place of 1.
** The loss pole is limited for passivity
Just below 'high_frequency_hz' the request is a steep drop
between two near frequencies; one pole then needs a DC gain above
1. The pole is kept no darker than the one at which DC gain
reaches 0.999999, so the loop keeps its T60 at f0 and gives up
the high-frequency loss. Before this a 3949 Hz note was 8 cents
off and nearly silent. When the discriminant is negative the
pole is -0.99 (then limited the same way), not 0, so the filter
does not jump from darkest to flat.
** "Log-linear in log2(f0)" read as log-log
The log of T60 (and of B) is linear in log2(f0): a power law
through the anchors. Linear T60 in log2(f0) goes negative a few
octaves above 1760 Hz. B must therefore be positive (validated
in (0, 0.1]).
** The dispersion chain is dropped when it would not fit
If its delay would leave the tuning allpass under its minimum,
the loop skips it and stays in tune.
** Two more files: 'first_order.rs', 'noise.rs'
The first-order filter (coefficients, state, magnitude, phase
delay, the allpass and one-pole lowpass designs) is shared by the
loop and the exciter, and the junction's lowpass will use it.
'noise.rs' is the LCG the body jitter and the pluck share.
** 'add_table' folds a long table into the loop
The table (one period plus a 60 ms knock) is longer than the
delay line; it wraps and adds onto itself, which equals streaming
for a lossless loop. Slice 3's junction writes 'excitation_i' per
sample and may stream instead. Folding raises the arriving peak
at high pitches (worst seen 1.17 at 22.05 kHz, pick_energy 0.5).
** Exciter details the plan left open
Position comb applied circularly over the one-period table (as
the loop sees it; it also cancels the strike's DC). Knock
normalized to unit energy, so 'knock_mix' is a level relative to
the dry table. Table length 'P + K - 1' (full convolution).
Strike pulses are 'sin^2' at half-sample centers, so a one-sample
pulse is not zero.
** Body details
Jitter up to 0.3 of the log spacing either way (centers keep
their order); modes at or above 0.45 fs are left out; resonator
'b0 (1 - z^-2)' with 'b0 = (1 - r^2) / 2' (peak gain near 1).
** Values the plan did not give
Howl level 0.3 and delay 5 ms (gain 0, so off). Validation upper
bounds of my choosing: unison_detune_cents 100, B 0.1,
dispersion_sections 16, pick_energy 1, strike_pulses 8,
knock_ms 1000, ex_post 60000 ms, body.mode_count 128,
modal.mode_count 32; pulses plus gaps must fit in one period.
** 'energy()' sums the in-flight window
Only the 'N' samples in flight, not the ring's spent slots.
