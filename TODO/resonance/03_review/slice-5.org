| drive_limit | 0.1 s  | 0.3 s  | 0.9 s  |
| 0.4         | 0.0609 | 0.1051 | 0.0560 |

discussion's reviewers suggested); I changed no default.

* Deviations from the plan's "how", with reasons
** The pedal moves the rate from the string's own decay
The plan writes 'rate = growth_rate_per_second_max * tri *
pedal^e' and 'k = max(0, exp(rate / f0) - g)'. With 'rate'
never negative, 'exp(rate / f0) >= 1 > g', so 'k' is at least
'1 - g' anywhere in the window: a heel pedal would hold a note
at constant level, which contradicts "the pedal at 0 decays as
without the pedal" and makes the 'max(0, ...)' and "below the
natural loss 'k' is 0" vacuous. Both only make sense if 'rate'
can reach the natural decay rate 'lambda = -f0 ln g'. So 'rate
= growth_max * f - lambda * (1 - f)', 'f' the energy factor: 'k'
is exactly 0 at a heel pedal, a light pedal lengthens the
sustain, and a full pedal at the window's peak grows every
register at 'growth_rate_per_second_max'. 'k' keeps the plan's
formula. 'energy.rs' states this in its module comment, with
the note that the drive is added after the loss, so the loop's
gain is 'g (1 + k)', not 'g + k' (a second-order difference).
** The history is a fixed 1 ms grid
Each grid point holds the position of the block that contains
it, so the pre-charge's step ('dt' = 1 ms) and cost do not
depend on the callback length. 10002 slots. The pre-charge
walks back from birth multiplying the decay by one constant per
step instead of an 'exp' per point.
** The pre-charge fills the delay line, not a whole period
'Loop::add_table' wraps a table longer than the delay line onto
itself, and the line is a few samples shorter than a period
(the filters hold the rest). The waveshape is therefore the
delay line's length of the shape, starting at phase 0, its peak
normalized to 'A0 / unison_count' over those samples. The
missing few samples are trimmed by the loss filter at once.
** A string voice keeps its birth's energy parameters
So a reload changes the window and drive of notes born after it
only, as "Hot reload" says, without any per-voice bookkeeping in
the renderer.
** A monome on the role is rejected, not ignored
Per the brief's preference; the message says the pedal is
rig-wide. Every taper knob is rejected by name.
** The pedal thread writes NaN when no pedal holds the role
So a reload that removes or rebinds the energy pedal never
leaves the engine driving from a stale position.
** The growth and window tests strike at a tenth of the limit
'pick_energy = drive_limit / 10', computed from the params. At
the default strike the limiter, not the drive, decides the
shape (see the measurement above), and the test is about the
drive. The window test averages energy over 50 ms: block by
block the delay line's sum of squares of a peaky wave wobbles a
percent or two, being a few samples short of a period.
