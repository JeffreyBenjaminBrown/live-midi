** What it rendered
A throwaway integration test, deleted before the commit. Ten
voices: a plain attack; a pluck decay; two note_offs mid-render;
a pending attack (relaunched twice); a glide up, later retargeted
down; a pedal slide, retargeted, then released; a factored pulse,
rate changed mid-note; grid_gain and global_pedal_gain slews,
retargeted; a timbre xfade, restarted mid-render; abs and rel AM
and FM, rel FM through zero; nonunit fader and new-pedal gains.
About 1 s per group, edits between 512-frame blocks, the final
voice count appended to catch the reaping. Once through
'BlockRenderer::new(2)' with 'render_with_distortion' (auto
makeup, two dirty voices per group), once through
'BlockRenderer::new(1)' with 'render'.
** Why the voices are grouped
'VoiceMap' is a 'HashMap' with a random seed per process, so its
iteration order, and hence the f32 summation order, differs
between runs. Each render therefore holds at most two voices per
bus, where 'a + b == b + a' exactly. Two compares before the
refactor confirmed the buffers are stable across processes.
** The check is sensitive
Multiplying the gain by '(1 + f32::EPSILON)' made the compare
fail; reverting restored equality.

* Deviations from the plan's "how"
** 'VoiceStep' lives in 'sawwave/types.rs'
That module's header says it holds all the data shapes and no
functions; 'step_voice_controls' is in 'voices.rs' right after
its caller 'accumulate_voices'.
** The LFO phase advance stays in 'accumulate_voices'
The four AM/FM phases feed only the sawwave modulators, and
keeping them after the control step keeps the old order exactly.
The strings engine leaves them unadvanced; nothing reads them
there. The doc comment of 'step_voice_controls' says so.
** A dead voice's step is all zeros
'alive: false' returns 'factored_pulse' 0 and 'gain' 0, and
nothing past the envelope is stepped (as the old 'retain'
closure returned before the glide).
** One comment outside the plan's file list changed
'surfaces_runtime/synth.rs' line 92 named 'accumulate_voices' as
where the volume chain is multiplied; it now names
'step_voice_controls'. A pointer fix in a comment, not an engine
change, so "the grid threads do not know which engine renders"
still holds.
** The crossfade's old-timbre gain is still computed inline
'b_old' uses 'x.from.gain' in place of 'timbre.gain', so it
cannot reuse 'step.gain' without reassociating the product.
