* Measurements behind the thresholds
** Howl growth
At 'howl.gain' 20 and 'level' 0.003, a struck 220 Hz note holds
an energy of 17.22 at 1 s and 24.91 at 3 s. Its peak over 10 s
is 0.504 and its RMS at 9.9 s is 0.363. Without the howl it
holds 0.156 and then 0.0001. In the reload test (the howl on at
0.5 s, off at 3 s) the energy is 10.14 at 1 s, 28.20 at 3 s and
0.035 at 5 s.
** The howl's threshold
A lone 220 Hz note at the defaults sustains between gain 1.5 and
1.8; the rig's formula predicts 1.65. At 880 Hz the 5 ms delay
puts the fundamental out of phase, and it sustains between 2.2
and 3, on another partial.
** Why the default level is 0.003
At gain 20 and 10 s, notes at 55, 220, 880, 1760 and 3000 Hz:
*** level 0.3
Every register is pinned at the clamp (peak 0.95).
*** level 0.03
Most notes are at the clamp.
*** level 0.01
RMS from 0.09 to 0.83; 220 Hz peaks at the clamp.
*** level 0.003
RMS from 0.15 to 0.36, peaks from 0.21 to 0.50, except 880 Hz
(RMS 0.027, the delay's dead spot).
** Gain 0 is the junction alone
A one-time check: the harness's five scenes, rendered on the rig
and with the defaults before the slice's code, are
byte-identical to the renders after it (10 WAVs), and again after
the harness change. The test asserts the same sample for sample
across levels and delays at gain 0.
** Modal pitch and T60
At 3000 Hz the measured pitch is 3000.083 Hz (+0.048 cents).
Alone, filled with one period of its fundamental, it falls 60 dB
in 1.1202 s against a curve value of 1.1202 s (+0.001 percent);
at 2500 Hz, 1.2377 s against 1.2378 s. Every mode's pole radius
gives its curve T60 within 1e-3.
** The level normalization
No measured factor: each mode's gain is 'beta = 2 f0 /
sample_rate', the Fourier coefficient of a loop's period-'P'
impulse train. So the bank is the loop's own response, partial
for partial, and the junction, the drive and the howl see a
modal voice exactly as they see a loop. The 'modal.rs' header
derives it. Measured, over the first 30 ms the modal fundamental
is 1.029, 1.034 and 1.041 times the loop voice's at 2500, 3000
and 3500 Hz. With a near-rigid bridge (r = 1e7) the ratios are
1.005 to 1.009, and with one unison 1.001 to 1.002. What remains
at r = 3000 is the bridge: see "Deviations".
** Brightness against loops
Over the first 0.2 s a modal note's total RMS is 1.42 to 1.71
times a loop voice's at the same pitch (peaks 1.03 to 1.24
times). The spec clamps the upper modes' decay at 'T60 * ratio'
above 'high_frequency_hz', while a loop's one-pole loss keeps
darkening there. I measured the alternative, extending the
log-linear line past the reference: 0.65 to 1.0 times, with the
second partial gone at 3500 Hz. That is too dark, so I kept the
clamp.
** Passivity with modal members
Three struck loop voices (750, 1000, 1500 Hz), two struck modal
voices (2250, 3000 Hz) and a silent one at 3000 Hz share one
bridge. The largest rise of the quarter-second mean energy is 0
at r = 1, 50 and 500. The silent modal voice holds 3.5e-5 to
6.2e-5 at 0.5 s (sympathy). The header of 'modal.rs' bounds 'Re
G > -0.45' for a modal member, which makes it a passive loop in
the junction's sense.

* Deviations from the plan's how
** The howl path lives in its own file
'strings/howl.rs' holds 'HowlPath'; the renderer wires it into
'couple_through_junction', which gained a 'howl' argument. Its
ring is sized once, at construction, for 20 ms, so a reload
never re-sizes it. Turning the path on clears the ring.
** The default howl.level is 0.003, not 0.3
0.3 pins every note at the clamp at gain 20 (above). The
validation of 'howl.level' is now [0, 1], as the plan says.
** Mode 1 sits at f0
The literal 'k f0 sqrt(1 + B k^2)' puts mode 1 7.8 cents sharp
at 3000 Hz and would fail the 2-cent test. The modes are
'k f0 sqrt((1 + B k^2) / (1 + B))', so the fundamental is at
'f0', as a loop's tuning puts it.
** Where the modal voice and the voice enum live
'ModalVoice' is in 'strings/modal.rs', 'VoiceKind' in
'voice.rs'. The two voices' shared excitation read-out became
'voice::ExcitationStream'. 'StringsRenderer::string_voice'
returns only a voice of loops, as before; the new
'voice_kind(id)' inspects either kind.
** The modal pre-charge streams through the input
One period of the waveshape is added into the dry table at
birth, where a loop adds it into its delay line.
** The harness has both a scene gain and --howl
The 'howl' scene sets gain 20; '--howl GAIN' overrides every
scene, as '--pedal' does.
** One junction member drains half as fast as two unisons
A modal note is one member, but a two-unison loop note is two
members moving together, so a modal note loses half as much to
the bridge. Derived at 2000 Hz and r = 3000: a T60 of about 1.1 s
against 0.9 s for loops. The rig's modal math says so. A fix
that needs no decision would count a modal voice as
'unison_count' members, each carrying '1 / n' of its output; I
left it, because the plan says the modal voice is one member.
