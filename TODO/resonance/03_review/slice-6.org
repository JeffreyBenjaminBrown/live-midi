** 804fa61 the drive limit rises with the pedal; unisons as one
The two corrections carried from slice 5. 'energy.rs': the
ebow drive is 'k * A * tanh(v / A)' with 'A = drive_limit *
pedal ^ e' (new 'energy::pedal_power'); 'drive' and
'ebow_drive' take the pedal's power. 'EnergyParams::default()
.drive_limit' is 0.8. 'voice.rs': 'StringVoice::write_back'
computes one drive sample per voice ('drive_of_the_voice') from
the mean of its loops' arriving waves and the mean of their
round-trip gains at the voice's 'f0', and adds it to every loop;
'drive_into_loop' is gone. 'renderer.rs' hands each voice its
pedal power beside its energy factor. Module and doc comments
updated. Two new tests: the drive saturates at its limit times
the pedal power; a full pedal keeps a voice's unisons in phase.

** The new coherence test
Heard power over stored energy of two default unisons (1.2
cents) relative to one loop's, at 0.6 s under a 1 s ex-post leg:
1.717 under a full pedal, 0.481 under none (2 is fully in phase).
With slice 5's per-loop drive restored in a scratch copy, the
full pedal gave 0.452: the drive alone did nothing for the phase.
The test asserts above 1.5 and above the undriven case. At 10
cents of detune the unisons do not lock (the drive's coupling is
weaker than the detune once the triangle falls), so the test
uses the default detune.
** The saturation test
At 220 Hz the saturated drive equals 'k * drive_limit * pedal ^
e' to 1e-4 at pedal 1 and 0.5 ('k' 4.37e-2 and 1.08e-2), and a
small wave takes 'k * v'.

* Deviations from the plan's "how", with reasons
** The mock test reads a test-only voice-map slot
A mock grid shows only LEDs, and the brief asks that the test
see voices in the map. 'run()' builds its voice map locally, so
'surfaces_runtime/mod.rs' gained a '#[cfg(test)]' static,
'VOICES_OF_THE_LATEST_RUN', set right after the map is built
(the tests that read it hold 'MOCK_LOCK'). Non-test builds are
unchanged; the engine is not involved, so "the grid threads do
not know which engine renders" still holds.
** The mock test's shape
Two grids (ports 19240/19241), then one (19242/19243), as the
brief orders. Two grids: a press strikes a voice; the handle
tap strikes nothing; after release the same 'VoiceId' rings
under a 'SurfaceDrone' key; OFF releases it; the shift pad's
rightward arrow (found by 'shift_for_cell') makes the same cell
strike a pitch higher by '2 ^ (register_delta / edo)'. One grid:
it registers, its mode switch boots as the rig says, a press
sounds and a release lets it go. Every cell, offset and step
comes from the resolved settings. Null audio, so no renderer
runs and nothing is reaped; "released" is 'target_env' 0.
** The bridge's example decay times are measured
The plan's UPDATED note gives about 42 s at 110 Hz and 2.6 s at
1760 Hz for 'r = 3000'. The formula for a two-unison note alone,
'T60_bridge = ln(1000) (N + r) / (2 n f0)', gives 47 s and 2.9
s; a render with the string's own loss removed measured 46 s,
13 s at 440 Hz and 5.5 s at 1760 Hz (the damping lowpass lags at
high pitch, which softens the bridge's loss). The rig gives the
formula and the measured values, at 'r' 3000, 300 and 50, and
the lone-loop factor '1 - 2 / (N + r)' beside the in-phase
'1 - 2 n / (N + r)'.
** The coherent drive's gain
The drive gain 'k' comes from the voice's 'f0' and the mean of
its loops' round-trip gains (the unisons' detune moves them a
little). The plan does not say which; this is one evaluation per
voice per sample instead of one per loop.
** The rig's layout
'ELEM sinks' sits directly under the rig's title headline,
without volpedal's 'audio' grouping, to keep the parameter tree
from reaching nine levels. The sink's own keys (sample rate,
buffer, amplitude, release) sit under two small grouping
headlines outside the two named groups, since they are not
string parameters. 'parameters you might also enjoy' carries
only the one-line body. Unisons and dispersion are in the first
group, as Jeff discussed both.
** rigs/README.org's reload sentence
It said the whole sink hot-reloads. 'release_secs',
'sample_rate' and 'buffer_frames' are not in 'LiveParams', so
they need a restart; the sentence now says so, as the readme
does.
