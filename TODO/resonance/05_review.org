** What the user sees differently
*** By decision
A rig may give any pedal in the 'string_energy' or
'string_parameters' role a list of targets. Target values are
linear in the raw travel; 'min' above 'max' inverts; equal
pins. A target on an 'energy' key shapes the next note, not
the sounding one. 'square_dance = false' silences the circling
cell; it needs a restart, like 'flash_sustained'.
'energy.ex_post_energizing_floor' above 0 keeps a held pedal
feeding a note for as long as it sounds. The harness takes
'--pedal2' and renders 'squeal'.
*** By accident
None found. Every 'cpal_synth' rig and Jeff's own strings rig
behave as before; the knock change renders within 1.5e-5.
