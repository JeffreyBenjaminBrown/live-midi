* minor design choices made
- Branch =monome-looper= off =main= (branch-first; reversible).
- Looper validation is an explicit =validate_looper= fn rather than a generic
  =WindowGroup= entry: it can express what the group mechanism cannot (exact
  counts, the >=2x2 display, =remap_center= bounds). The existing
  "remap"/"record"/"scale" groups are left exactly as they were.
- The looper's *required core* is one =loop_slots=, one =loop_display=, and the
  three =loop_control= kinds; the shift pad, mode toggle, copy, and undo are
  *optional* add-ons (a looper can be configured without them). Slightly more
  modular than 6_plan's "include loop_remap_undo in the group."
- Added a =kind_name()= helper to =MonomeWindowConfig= (for the inventory print).
- Looper configs forbid *orphan monomes* (every declared monome must be used by
  some window); scoped to looper configs, so existing configs are unaffected.
- The =NoteSink= owns its *own* =VoiceMap=, keyed by the =Accreted= =VoiceSource=
  used purely as an opaque per-pitch handle (=render_block= ignores the key). So
  the sawwave engine is reused *unchanged*. One ref-counted voice per pitch
  across sources (live + a slot can share a pitch).
- Shift-pad sign convention: the octave corners are anchored by =1_vision= (top-
  left = down an octave / sounds lower). The arrows follow consistently
  (down/right raise, up/left lower); the *left/right* pitch direction is the one
  bit the vision left unstated -- chose "right = higher." Trivially flippable if
  it feels wrong on the grid.
- The looper uses its *own* small =LoopEvent= type rather than reusing
  remap_runtime's =pub(crate)= =RecordedEvent= (which is entangled and not visible
  outside =remap_runtime=). Reuses the *shape*, not the type -- zero changes to
  =record.rs=, per the no-regressions invariant.
- The boundary cut (notes held at the loop end) is implemented by *playback
  releasing everything on wrap*, not by storing end-of-loop note-offs. Simpler,
  and it sidesteps the =normalize_events= "drop elapsed >= duration" trap the
  critique flagged.
