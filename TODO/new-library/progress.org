* choices determining the future, recorded rather than asked
Per =when-to-stop.org=: these were easily reversed, so I took them.

** a voice has a home, and a drag re-homes it
Your retrigger rule needs a voice to be findable at a monomekey with no finger on it,
and your story needs the dragging finger to end up holding the voice. Both fall out of
giving a voice a =home= monomekey that the drag moves. The alternative was a separate
index from monomekey to voice, which is the same thing with an extra place to forget to
update -- and forgetting to update an index is precisely what caused the bug where a
dragged voice died on release.

** Held has four states rather than three
=Finger=, =EditDeferred=, =Sustained=, =Fading=. =EditDeferred= exists only because of
your key-up rule. I considered a boolean "the finger is up but we are waiting", and the
state is clearer: it makes the illegal combinations unrepresentable rather than merely
unlikely.

** a faded voice keeps its entry until the synth retires it
=Fading= is a state rather than a deletion, so the synth can ring it out over its
release while nothing can address it. The old library did this with a whole separate
=RETIRED_BASE= keyspace and a uniquifier counter.

** sustaining a voice does not check whether one already sounds that pitch
The old code did, and released the incoming voice rather than storing it. That is the
bug your collisions paragraph exposed, so the check is gone rather than fixed.
