* how both work
The SoftStep is put in *tether mode*, where it streams each pad's *4 pressure
sensors* as Control Change messages, refreshed only *~every 10 ms*. The runtime
*sums a pad's 4 sensors* (0..508) and runs a tiny per-pad state machine:

1. *Onset* -- the sum rising above =on_sum= (20) FIRES immediately
   (=Idle -> Watching=), even a one-sample tap, at whatever *pressure* the sum
   already reads (peak-so-far / =pressure_full_scale=, a continuous 0.0..1.0 --
   no 1..127 quantization step) -- *unless* the same pad fired <
   =debounce_ms= (100 ms) ago, in which case it is suppressed and must release
   first.
2. *Attack window* -- for =attack_ms= (14 ms) after onset it keeps watching: a
   strictly higher peak emits a *Revise*, ramping the sounding voice up to the
   new pressure.
3. *Hold* -- once the window closes the pressure is locked in; the pad stays
   Held until it releases.
4. *Re-arm* -- the pad won't fire again until its sum falls back below
   =off_sum= (20).

For a *first* strike, one gate governs it: the sum must *exceed 20*. For a
*re*-strike on the same pad, two more apply: the pad must have *released*
(fallen below 20) since its last fire, and the new onset must land at least
*100 ms* after that fire.

"How hard" = the peak sum seen during the attack window, over
=pressure_full_scale= (a continuous 0.0..1.0, no velocity quantization step),
then mapped to a *loudness* spread across a 20 dB range (pressure 1.0 = full,
pressure 0.0 = -20 dB).

The two constants most relevant to the symptoms: =on_sum= and =off_sum= are *both
20* (there is *no hysteresis gap*), and =debounce_ms= guards 100 ms. The symptom
walk-throughs live in the baseline snapshot:
[[file:historical/before-these-changes.org::*Where "no hit" comes from][Where "no hit" comes from]] and
[[file:historical/before-these-changes.org::*Where "double hit" comes from][Where "double hit" comes from]]
(written against the older velocity model, but the mechanism still reads true).

The old SoftStep device only refreshes its sensors ~every 10 ms
(which rationalizes the 14 ms attack window).
The new one (arriving tomorrow) might be faster.

Loudness is ~logarithmic, over a 20 db range.
Pressure is derived entirely from the sensor sum; the KMSS itself has no velocity.

* constants this code uses

| constant              | value  | where                                | role                                                              |
|-----------------------+--------+---------------------------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------------|
| =on_sum=              | 20     | softstep.toml:10                     | onset: sum must exceed this to *start* a hit                      |
| =off_sum=             | 20     | softstep.toml:11                     | release: sum must drop below this to *re-arm*                     |
| =attack_ms=           | 14 ms  | softstep.toml:12                     | after firing on onset, how long to keep watching for a higher peak |
| =pressure_full_scale= | 460    | softstep.toml:13                     | pad sum (0..508) that maps to full pressure (1.0)                 |
| =gain_db_range=       | 20 dB  | softstep.toml:14                     | loudness spread from pressure 0.0 to pressure 1.0                 |
| =debounce_ms=         | 100 ms | softstep.toml:15                     | min gap between two fires on the *same* pad                       |
| poll interval         | 1 ms   | mod.rs:360                            | how often matured hits are checked (device scan ~10 ms dominates) |
| sink =amplitude=      | 1.6    | 2-monomes_kmss-drums.org:134          | drum master gain, applied at mix, clamped [-1,1]                  |
| device scan           | ~10 ms | firmware (fixed)                     | sensors only refresh ~100 Hz -- the latency/aliasing floor        |

=on_sum=/=off_sum=/=attack_ms=/=pressure_full_scale=/=gain_db_range=/=debounce_ms=
are all fields of =SoftstepParams= (=buried/midi_pulse/rig.rs=); the values
above are what =rigs/softstep.toml= actually sets (not =decode.rs= consts --
=decode.rs= just consumes whatever =SoftstepParams= it is given).

*Note the missing hysteresis gap:* =on_sum= and =off_sum= are *both 20*. There is no
dead-band between "start a hit" and "re-arm." (The Python meter is a *separate*
source of truth and has drifted further: =tools/softstep/meter/config.toml=
now uses =on-sum = 5= / =off-sum = 2= / =pressure-full-scale = 300=. See
[[file:historical/before-these-changes.org::*meter.py disagrees with the runtime][meter.py disagrees with the runtime]]
and =tools/softstep/meter/synth-v-meter.org=.)
