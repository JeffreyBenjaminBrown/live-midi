* 2. What is a "group" across *ten* pads? (board-wide vs per-pad)

Spec (singular): /"if the sum-of-four from *a pad* falls below it, the events that
just happened are considered 'a group'."/ But the group *number* =X= is a single
global counter and the messages are singular ("new group ... X"), which fits *one*
stream of groups, not ten parallel per-pad streams. The two readings diverge sharply:

- *Per-pad* (literal): every time any single pad releases, that pad's recent events
  close as a group. Problem: releasing the kick mid-phrase would cut a group while
  the snare is still going, and two feet would produce two overlapping "groups"
  fighting over one global counter =X=.
- *Board-wide* (what I chose): a group is a *burst of whole-board activity*. It
  *starts* when any pad first rises to/above the threshold from a quiet board, and
  *ends* when *all* pads have fallen back below it. One group at a time, one clean
  =X= sequence, and a two-foot stomp is one group containing both feet's events.

*My default (coded):* *board-wide*. A group = from "board goes active" to "board
goes quiet," and its =X.csv= holds *every* pad's events in that window.

*Confirm board-wide*, or tell me you really want per-pad (then =X= numbering and
"the events that just happened" need per-pad semantics defined).

* 3. Group end needs a *hold time*, or brief dips will shred every phrase

* 4. What goes *in* the CSV? (schema unspecified)

Spec says write "the events that just happened" to =X.csv= but not what a row is or
what columns. Since the whole point is diagnosing *no-hit / double-hit*, I logged the
richest useful trace: *one row per incoming sensor update* (each of a pad's 4 sensors
arrives as its own CC), so you can replay the exact pressure ramp and see where a hit
did or didn't fire.

*My default (coded) columns:*
: t_mono, pad, base_cc, s0, s1, s2, s3, sum4, state, event, velocity
- =t_mono= -- monotonic seconds (device clock via =amidi -T monotonic=).
- =s0..s3= -- the pad's four raw sensors; =sum4= their sum (0..508).
- =state= -- =idle= / =rising= / =held= at that instant.
- =event= -- blank, or =onset= / =fire= / =release= when the state machine crosses one.
- =velocity= -- filled only on the =fire= row (the 1..127 that would sound).

*Tell me if you want fewer columns* (e.g. only one row per detected hit) *or more*
(e.g. the nav-pad / expression CCs too). This is the schema I'm least sure you'll
like, and it's trivial to slim.
