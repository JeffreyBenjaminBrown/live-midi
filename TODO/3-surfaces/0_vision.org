I need a few changes to '2-monomes_58-8-1_kmss-drums.toml'.
* the four waveform buttons should be identical on both monomes
Each monome has four buttons in the top-righto control the other's waveform.
On one of them, the active one is lit solid, and the others are dim.
On the other, the active one is lit solid and the rest are off.
They should instead be the same.

The problem here might be that one monome understands dimness,
and the other doesn't. If that's the case, we need to use flashing
to fake the dimness. I know that dimness is in fact achieved by rapid flashing anyway,
on a monome that permits dimness controls, so please look up online what duty cycle
and frequency correspond to the different dimness levels. Let me know
if you think this will be a performance bottleneck.
* there should be a volume control
In the top row, the 12 so-far unused buttons should be for volume.
Let's make it a logarithmic control, spread over a 30 db range.
The active volume position should be lit, and the rest unlit.
The active volume position should begin at button 10
(which leaves 5 spots to the right for headroom).
* notes from either monome should be reflected in the other
Currently if you press a note on one monome,
its enharmonic equivalents light up across all octaves on that monome.
Its enharmonic equivalents should also light up on the other monome.
* notes should leave trails
Let's keep a history of the most recent 7 notes that were pressed,
regardless of whether they are currently being pressed,
and display them all dimly. If they are currently being pressed,
they should display fully lit (fully lit clobbers dimly lit).
