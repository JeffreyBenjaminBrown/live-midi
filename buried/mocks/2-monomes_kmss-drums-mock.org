headlines nest tables and array-of-table elements; any other headline just groups and

* 2-monomes_kmss-drums-mock
** PARAM version = 1
** PARAM id = "2-monomes_kmss-drums-mock"
** PARAM title = "Two monomes + KMSS drums (mock grids)"
** TABLE surfaces
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Headless mock of the surfaces runtime: two mock 16x16 grids with the scroll
pads, self-control waveform strips, and the accrete (sustain) button trios, driven with MIDI_PULSE_NO_AUDIO (no
sound card). The KMSS drumkit is deliberately OMITTED here: entering tether
mode shells out to `amidi` against a real device, which a headless test has
no way to satisfy. This still exercises the two-grid discovery/bind, LED
output, and press routing -- the device layer the pure tests can't. The runtime
is still selected as "surfaces" (two edo grids on distinct monomes).
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Shared recent-note trail -- mirror the real rig so mock play matches it.
*** PARAM trail_clobber_radius = 27
1/27 of an octave (~44 cents); bigger = tighter
*** PARAM trails_max = 9
*** PARAM slide_candidate_window_ms = 1000
Slide (the slide_toggle windows): a new note may glide from a note released
within this many ms...
*** PARAM slide_duration_ms = 100
...taking this long to arrive at the new pitch.
*** PARAM tap_tempo_window_ms = 2000
Tap tempo (the tap_tempo_pad): two taps at most this far apart set the tempo.

** ELEM sinks
*** PARAM id = "synth"
*** PARAM kind = "cpal_synth"
*** PARAM sample_rate = 48000
*** PARAM buffer_frames = 128
*** PARAM amplitude = 0.15
*** PARAM attack_secs = 0.003
*** PARAM release_secs = 0.050
*** PARAM sustain_level = 0.35
Pluck envelope: a fresh strike peaks, then decays exponentially toward
sustain_level x peak (time constant decay_secs), so new notes ring out over held
ones -- a rough plucked-string curve that plateaus instead of dying (held notes
and accrete drones keep ringing at the sustain).
sustain_level: fraction of the strike peak; 0.2..0.5 natural, >= 1.0 disables.
*** PARAM decay_secs = 0.5
decay_secs: the decay time constant; ~0.3 snappy, ~1.5 slow ring, <= 0 disables.
*** PARAM distortion_scale = 1.0
Distortion (per-monome; heard only while a grid's distortion_toggle is on): the
soft-clipper f(y) = y / (1 + |y/s|^k)^(1/k) on the switched-on grids' summed mix.
scale s: the asymptote; ~0.3 heavy, ~1.0 strong, 2-3 gentle, >=5 nearly clean.
*** PARAM distortion_shape = 2.0
shape k: elbow harshness; 1 gentlest, 2 smooth sweet spot, 4-8 hard-ish edge.

** ELEM monome_windows
The sustain ("accrete") buttons, bottom row left. One accrete BANK per monome:
this trio drives only this grid's bank, and only notes fingered on this grid can
join it (see TODO/misc.org "sustain (accrete)"):
(0,15) clear         -- silence + flush this grid's sustained notes (lit while pressed)
(1,15) needs_holding -- toggles whether accrete must be held (lit when on)
(2,15) accrete       -- hold (or, untoggled, tap) to sustain played/held notes;
they ring until cleared
(1,14) erase         -- accrete's mirror image (same needs_holding rule): pressed
pitches LEAVE the sustained set, each sounding until its
own finger lifts; when both are live, erase wins

** ELEM monome_windows
Feet accrete (key-down flips; PER-GRID): while on, the KMSS pedal triple mapped
to this grid -- 1/2/3 for the older (monobright) monome, 8/9/0 for the other --
mirrors this grid's accrete trio (clear / needs-holding / accrete) instead of
playing samples. The softstep can mirror one monome, both, or neither. Just
above the on-grid trio.
*** PARAM id = "feet-accrete-a"
*** PARAM monome = "a"
*** PARAM kind = "softstep_accretes_toggle"
*** PARAM rect = [0, 14, 0, 14]
** ELEM monome_windows
The polyrhythm pad, top-right: | x3 x2 tap | /3 /2 =1 |. Tap twice within
[surfaces].tap_tempo_window_ms to set the tempo (the tap cell blinks at 10% duty,
at the UNFACTORED tapped tempo); the factor buttons scale it exactly (2^a * 3^b).
=1 starts this grid's cycling (keeping the factor), snaps to unity on a second
press, and stops on a fast double-tap. Notes struck while a tempo is applied and
cycling is on pulse with a unipolar triangle at that tempo (fixed per note,
separate from any [[timbres]] AM).
*** PARAM id = "poly-a"
*** PARAM monome = "a"
*** PARAM kind = "tap_tempo_pad"
*** PARAM rect = [13, 0, 15, 1]
