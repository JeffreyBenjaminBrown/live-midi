This rig is written in our TOML-in-org format. The data lives in headlines titled
"PARAM key = value" (values are ordinary TOML syntax); "TABLE name" / "ELEM name"
headlines nest tables and array-of-table elements; any other headline just groups and
documents; and every body (like this one) is a human note the loader ignores. It loads
into the exact same rig as the old .toml did. Format reference (C-c C-o to follow):
[[file:README.org]]
* monome-looper-58-8-1-timbre-mock
** PARAM version = 1
** PARAM id = "monome-looper-58-8-1-timbre-mock"
** PARAM title = "Monome Looper 58 8 1 (timbre, mock grids)"
** ELEM tunings
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
The full timbre instrument (6_plan + 7_layout.org). Two timbre editors:
- EDO monome: the loop-timbre editor (target=loop), occluding the top of the
play grid. Select notes on the loop display, then dial params here to stamp
them; with no selection + a playing loop, hold a param to play-along.
- LOOPS monome: the live-timbre editor (target=live), with the whole looper
stack (slots, transport, copy/undo/remap, loop display) reflowing DOWN under
it. Both editors rest FOLDED (a 1-row strip); unfolding the loops editor
slides the looper stack down by 6 rows (7_layout.org). Edit X's timbre on the
not-X monome so X stays visible (6_plan 2.2).
Loops rects below are the UNFOLDED positions; the runtime starts folded.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

*** PARAM edo = 58
*** PARAM x_step = 8
*** PARAM y_step = 1
*** PARAM fundamental_hz = 80
** ELEM sinks

*** PARAM amplitude = 1.0
Unity pass-through: the per-note GAIN row is the volume control (6_plan 2.5). The
amplitude row's top cell = unity = 0.15 output; the live timbre boots there, so the
instrument starts at the loudest value the row can dial. (See the rigs/ twin.)
*** PARAM attack_secs = 0.003
*** PARAM release_secs = 0.050
*** PARAM oversample = 4
4x oversample + decimate so audio-rate AM/FM shaping doesn't alias. (rigs/ twin.)

The timbre editor, listed AFTER edo-grid so it occludes the top 7 rows. Row 0 is
the control row (fold | Sine Triangle Square Saw | arm | sustain | save-undo |
slots..), then amplitude, then AM amp/freq/shape and FM amp/freq. Row ranges are
the 6_plan 5 defaults, spelled out here to exercise the explicit-range path.
*** PARAM id = "edo-timbre"
*** PARAM monome = "edo"
*** PARAM kind = "timbre_editor"
*** PARAM target = "loop"
*** PARAM rect = [0, 0, 15, 6]
*** PARAM amplitude = { kind = "log_range", least = 0.0009, greatest = 0.15 }
*** PARAM am_amplitude = { kind = "linear", min = 0.0, max = 1.0 }
