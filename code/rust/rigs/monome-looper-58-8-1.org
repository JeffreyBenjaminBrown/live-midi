This rig is written in our TOML-in-org format. The data lives in headlines titled
"PARAM key = value" (values are ordinary TOML syntax); "TABLE name" / "ELEM name"
headlines nest tables and array-of-table elements; any other headline just groups and
documents; and every body (like this one) is a human note the loader ignores. It loads
into the exact same rig as the old .toml did. Format reference (C-c C-o to follow):
[[file:README.org]]
* monome-looper-58-8-1
** PARAM version = 1
** PARAM id = "monome-looper-58-8-1"
** PARAM title = "Monome Looper 58 8 1"
** ELEM tunings
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Two 16x16 grids, no piano. One is the EDO play/remap surface ("edo"), the
other is the LOOPS surface ("loops"). Both match select.size = [16,16]; the
runtime hands each rig monome a DISTINCT unclaimed grid in rig order
(so the first-discovered grid is "edo", the second "loops"). We don't care
which physical grid is which. See 6_plan.org.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
*** PARAM id = "main"
*** PARAM edo = 58
*** PARAM x_step = 8
*** PARAM y_step = 1
*** PARAM fundamental_hz = 80
** ELEM sinks
cpal sawwave sink, same params as monome-edo-sawwave. BOTH live play and loop
playback emit note events into this synth.
*** PARAM id = "saw"
*** PARAM kind = "cpal_synth"
*** PARAM sample_rate = 48000
*** PARAM buffer_frames = 128
*** PARAM amplitude = 0.15
*** PARAM attack_secs = 0.003
*** PARAM release_secs = 0.050
** TABLE looper
Looper-wide scalars (all ms unless noted).
*** PARAM clock_bpm = 300
metronome (4*75): transport snaps to the 200ms cycle; note-ons within a fixed 50ms snap
*** PARAM clock_duty = 0.1
fraction of each cycle the flashing transport button stays lit (a brief strobe)
*** PARAM cluster_display_ms = 100
notes within this of each other = one display column
*** PARAM flash_ms = 50
reflection / equivalent flash half-period (50 on / 50 off)
*** PARAM remap_center = [7, 7]
edo-grid cell meaning "unison" in group-transpose remap
** ELEM monomes
*** PARAM id = "edo"
*** PARAM listen_port = 9000
*** PARAM prefix = "/looper-edo"
*** PARAM select.size = [16, 16]
** ELEM monomes
*** PARAM id = "loops"
*** PARAM listen_port = 9001
*** PARAM prefix = "/looper-loops"
*** PARAM select.size = [16, 16]
** ELEM monome_windows
===========================================================================
GRID 1 -- "edo": play surface, loop reflection, remap pitch-picker.
===========================================================================
*** PARAM id = "edo-grid"
*** PARAM monome = "edo"
*** PARAM kind = "edo_note_grid"
*** PARAM rect = [0, 0, 15, 15]
*** PARAM tuning = "main"
*** PARAM sink = "saw"
** ELEM monome_windows
3x2 shift pad overlaid on the lower-right (listed AFTER edo-grid so it sits on
top). Arrows shift the pitch image by one CELL; the two top corners by an octave.
x=13         x=14        x=15
y=14  octave-down  shift-up    octave-up
y=15  shift-left   shift-down  shift-right
*** PARAM id = "edo-shift-pad"
*** PARAM monome = "edo"
*** PARAM kind = "edo_shift_pad"
*** PARAM rect = [13, 14, 15, 15]
** ELEM monome_windows
===========================================================================
GRID 2 -- "loops": slots, transport, loop display, remap controls.
===========================================================================
4x4 slot grid (16 slots). One is ACTIVE. The transport windows below overlay
its bottom row, so those three slots are unreachable ("no harm").
*** PARAM id = "loop-slots"
*** PARAM monome = "loops"
*** PARAM kind = "loop_slots"
*** PARAM rect = [0, 0, 3, 3]
** ELEM monome_windows
*** PARAM id = "loop-start"
begin recording into the active slot (overwrites it)
*** PARAM monome = "loops"
*** PARAM kind = "loop_control"
*** PARAM control = "start"
*** PARAM rect = [0, 3, 0, 3]
** ELEM monome_windows
*** PARAM id = "loop-stop"
stop the active slot's current activity -- record OR play
*** PARAM monome = "loops"
*** PARAM kind = "loop_control"
*** PARAM control = "stop"
*** PARAM rect = [1, 3, 1, 3]
** ELEM monome_windows
*** PARAM id = "loop-play"
stop recording (if any) and loop the active slot
*** PARAM monome = "loops"
*** PARAM kind = "loop_control"
*** PARAM control = "play"
*** PARAM rect = [2, 3, 2, 3]
** ELEM monome_windows
Remap-mode toggle: fine vs group transpose. Both are destructive; toggling back
to fine also ENDS a group-transpose session. Lit solid in fine (the default),
dim in group transpose.
*** PARAM id = "loop-remap-mode"
*** PARAM monome = "loops"
*** PARAM kind = "loop_remap_mode_toggle"
*** PARAM rect = [14, 3, 14, 3]
** ELEM monome_windows
Copy a slot to another: press this, then 'from' slot, then 'to' slot. Lit solid
while armed; pressing it again mid-gesture cancels (forgetting any picked slot).
*** PARAM id = "loop-copy"
*** PARAM monome = "loops"
*** PARAM kind = "loop_copy_button"
*** PARAM rect = [15, 2, 15, 2]
** ELEM monome_windows
One-press undo of the last loop remap (fine or group transpose). Its own looper kind --
shares the undo mechanism in code, not the entangled remap_undo_button kind.
*** PARAM id = "loop-remap-undo"
*** PARAM monome = "loops"
*** PARAM kind = "loop_remap_undo"
*** PARAM rect = [15, 3, 15, 3]
** ELEM monome_windows
The loop display compound: one rect derives a left row-picker column, a top
column-picker row, a reserved corner (0,5), and the main time x pitch area.
*** PARAM id = "loop-display"
*** PARAM monome = "loops"
*** PARAM kind = "loop_display"
*** PARAM rect = [0, 4, 15, 15]
