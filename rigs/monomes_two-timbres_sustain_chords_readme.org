* Claude's lengthier description
** Run

Connect either one or two 16x16 monomes.  From the repository root inside the
container run:

#+begin_src sh
pw-jack cargo run --bin midi_pulse -- monomes_two-timbres_sustain_chords
#+end_src

Either physical grid can be used alone.  With two, each grid has independent
notes, sustain, chord and loop banks, registers, controls, and loop clocks.
Ctrl-C blanks the grids and stops audio.

The shipped rig begins at 110 Hz in 41-EDO.  Right adds eight steps and down
adds one.  Edit mode, SoftSteps, expression pedals, and factored pulse are not
part of this rig.

** Layout

#+begin_example
y=0  SIN TRI SQUARE SAW  TARGET[1]  . . . . . . . .  SHIFT SHIFT SHIFT
y=1  -12  -2    +2  +12             . . . . . . . .  SHIFT SHIFT SHIFT

                       EDO PLAY SURFACE

y=14 C C C C C C C ACCRETE  | CLEAR SELECT L L L L L L
y=15 C C C C C C MODE ARM   | STOP START L L L L L PHASE
#+end_example

TARGET = timbre and volume target.
  Solid = loop, flashing = stored chords, off = fingered & sustained voices.
  There only are three (timbre, volume) pairs;
    all the distinct chords share the same pair, as do distinct loops.

The thirteen =C= cells are chord slots.  The eleven =L= cells are loop slots.
The dB labels are the default coarse and fine volume steps; the rig can change
them.  The four arrow cells in SHIFT move one grid cell.  Its two outer top
corners move one configured EDO octave and change meaning with TARGET as
described below.

** TARGET, waveform, and volume

TARGET cycles through three layers:

- black: fingered and sustained voices;
- dim: recalled chord voices; and
- solid: compact-loop playback voices.

Each layer remembers its own waveform choice.  Each waveform remembers a base
finger/sustain volume plus separate chord and loop offsets.  Thus a volume
change while TARGET is black also carries the other two layers' bases with it;
a change while TARGET is dim or solid moves only that layer relative to the
base.  Changes affect sounding voices immediately without restarting them.

With TARGET black, the two octave corners scroll the keyboard.  With TARGET
dim, they temporarily glide all sounding chord recalls by one configured EDO
octave.  With TARGET solid, they permanently transpose the current loop record
target, save it, and glide that slot if it is playing.  Loop octave edits do
nothing while recording.

** Sustain

Hold ACCRETE while playing.  Notes already held, and notes played while it is
held, join this grid's sustained set and keep ringing after finger release.
CLEAR removes only sustain: it does not end a held finger, a recalled chord, or
loop playback, and does not erase either stored bank.

At rest ACCRETE and CLEAR alternate in opposite halves of a one-second flash.
Holding either button makes that button solid.

** Chords

The thirteen slots store pitches only.  An empty slot is black, a populated
silent slot is dim, and a sounding slot is solid.

MODE begins solid (momentary).  In momentary mode, hold a populated slot to
sound it and release the cell to end that recall.  Press MODE to make it black
(toggle mode); then each slot press independently toggles that recall, so
several may remain on.  Returning to momentary mode releases all toggled
recalls.

To store a chord without a source chord slot, press ARM, play or sustain its
notes, and press a destination slot.  ARM flashes until the save.  Recalled
chords can also be sources: in momentary mode ARM overwrites the source slots
you are physically holding; in toggle mode it overwrites the latched-on source
slots.  Storage never captures loop playback or the tiny crowded-pitch offsets.

Chord slots persist by physical monome serial id in
=state/chord-slots/monomes_two-timbres_sustain_chords.toml=.  Older eight-slot
files retain their first eight slots and gain five empty ones.

** Compact loops

Loop slot 1 (the first =L= cell at =(10,14)=) is the record target at startup
and flashes.  Press SELECT and then any loop slot to choose another target;
SELECT flashes while waiting, and pressing SELECT again cancels.  A slot touch
used for selection never changes playback.

To make a first loop:

1. Press START.
2. Play, sustain, and/or recall local chords freely.
3. Press STOP at the cycle boundary.

START flashes while recording.  The first take's START-to-STOP span becomes its
fixed duration.  A populated target receives an overdub instead; its duration
and phase stay fixed.  Recording includes sources that were already sounding
at START, but excludes every loop-playback voice, so it cannot feed back.
When the target is also playing back during recording, its slot alternates
quickly between bright and dim (80 ms each) and never goes black.

A quick START then STOP shorter than =loop_clear_tap_ms= (300 ms initially)
clears only the target loop.  A longer take with no captured notes makes no
loop.  Interrupted recordings are not published.

Touch a populated loop slot to turn its playback on or off.  Many slots can
play together, each with its own duration and phase.  Touching the empty target
while its first take is being recorded toggles a pending request to begin
playing at STOP.  At other times an empty slot does nothing.

PHASE begins flashing in KEEP-RUNNING mode.  Once a slot has first played, its
clock keeps moving while muted, so a later touch rejoins its current phase.
Press PHASE to make it black (RESTART mode); each playback-on then begins at
phase zero.  Changing to RESTART also re-anchors sounding loops at zero through
the normal release and attack ramps.

Compact loops persist by physical serial id in
=state/compact-loops/monomes_two-timbres_sustain_chords.toml=.  Pitch and timing
are saved; targets, gates, phase, mode, timbre, and volume restart from their
normal defaults.

** Equal-pitch voice packing

Every simultaneous surface voice at the same nominal pitch remains independently
owned, including two enharmonic keyboard cells such as =(x,y)= and
=(x+1,y-8)= when the tuning makes them equal.  The rig initially separates such
voices by the first free offset in =0,+5,-5,+10,-10,...= cents.  Change
=crowded_pitch_planck_deviation= in the rig to choose another spacing, or set it
to zero to disable separation.  Offsets are live-performance state and are
never stored.
