#+title: Monomes: new-note and global volume pedals, opted into per grid

* Run
Connect one or two 16x16 monomes. For the pedals, run =tools/pedals/bridge.sh=
on the HOST first, in a terminal you leave open, every session. Then, from the
repository root inside the container:

#+begin_src sh
pw-jack cargo run --bin midi_pulse -- volpedal-newnotes-and-global_granular-sustain
#+end_src

Either physical grid can be used alone. The rig looks for the pedals only at
startup: if the bridge wasn't running then, it prints a red "expression pedals
skipped" line and plays on at full pedal volume until restarted. Working pedals
print "expression pedals: connected" instead. Ctrl-C blanks the grids
and stops audio.

* Layout
#+begin_example
       x=0   1   2   3                                 13    14    15
y=0  | SIN TRI SQR SAW  . . . . . . . . .            SHIFT SHIFT SHIFT
y=1  | -12  -2  +2 +12  . . . . . . . . .            SHIFT SHIFT SHIFT
     |
     |                   EDO PLAY SURFACE
     |
y=15 | NEW GLOBAL . . . .  OFF  ON  TOGGLE  . . . . . . .
       x=0   x=1          x=6  x=7  x=8
#+end_example
NEW and GLOBAL opt this grid into the two pedals (see "The pedals").
Everything else is play surface: 41-EDO from 110 Hz, eight steps per cell
rightward and one step per cell downward. A control at rest flashes dim on the
shared 150 ms clock; a lit control is solid bright. A held note is solid bright,
and a sustained note no finger holds flashes bright on that same clock.

* The pedals
** what they do
A note's volume at any moment is its START level times the GLOBAL level, times
the volume keys and the rest.
*** the new-note pedal (MPC-20 channel 1)
It sets the level each note starts at. The note keeps that level for its whole
life, held or sustained; moving the pedal never changes a note already sounding.
Striking a sustained pitch again starts a new note, at the pedal's current level.
*** the global pedal (MPC-20 channel 2)
It scales every note live, held or sustained, gliding over 20 ms so sweeps are
smooth.
** opting in, per grid
Each grid has two buttons at the bottom-left. NEW (x=0) lit means notes struck
on this grid start at the new-note pedal's level; dim means they start at
unity. GLOBAL (x=1) lit means every note on this grid rides the global pedal;
dim means they glide to unity and ignore it. Both start lit. Each grid's buttons
are its own, so one grid can follow a pedal while the other ignores it.
** the taper
Heel is silent and toe is unity. The top 90% of the travel is dB-linear over
50 dB, and the first 10% fades linearly to exact silence. Both knobs are in
the rig and reload live with =r= then Enter. Until a pedal sends its first CC
it counts as unity, so an unplugged pedal mutes nothing.
** cabling
If the pedals arrive the other way round, swap the two =channel= values in the
rig.

* Sustain
Each grid has its own sustained set. OFF, ON and TOGGLE act on their own grid
only.
** the handle: the cell below a note
Press the cell directly below a sounding note to toggle that note's sustain.
The press never sounds. Sustaining a held note keeps it ringing after you lift
your finger. Pressing the handle of a sustained note un-sustains it, which ends
it unless a finger still holds it. The cell above a note is an ordinary note.

The handle is geometric, so it stays put when the tuning changes. With this
tuning it is also the pitch one EDO step above the note, which therefore can't
be played from that cell while the note sounds. A note on the bottom row, or
directly above OFF, ON or TOGGLE, has no handle. The rig can move the handle
to another side with =sustain_handle=.
** OFF
Ends every sustained note on this grid that no finger holds. Held notes keep
sounding, now on the new pedal.
** ON
Notes already held, and notes played while ON is live, join the sustained set.
** TOGGLE WHAT ON MEANS
Bright means ON is momentary: hold it. Dim means ON latches: tap it on, tap it
off. It starts bright.

* Timbre and volume
The four waveform cells and the four volume cells work as in the two-timbres
rig, per grid. A waveform press re-timbres this grid's fingered and sustained
voices in place. Each volume cell moves the current waveform's level by -12,
-2, +2 or +12 dB, from -12 dB at start. The pedals multiply on top of that.

* Shift
The four arrow cells move the visible register one cell. The two outer top
corners move it one octave.

* Deliberately not here
No chord storage, no loops, no tone-target cell, no edit mode, no SoftSteps,
no square or diamond dances.
