#+title: Monomes: coupled strings under two pedals

* Run
Connect one or two 16x16 monomes and two EX-P pedals to the
MPC-20, on channels 1 and 2. Run 'tools/pedals/bridge.sh' on the
HOST first, in a terminal you leave open, every session. Then,
from the repository root inside the container:

#+begin_src sh
pw-jack cargo run --bin midi_pulse -- two-pedals_monome
#+end_src

Either physical grid can be used alone. The rig looks for the
pedals only at startup: if the bridge wasn't running then, it
prints a red "expression pedals skipped" line and plays on with
no pedals (every note is struck at the rig's own level, nothing
blooms, nothing squeals) until restarted. Working pedals print
"expression pedals: connected" instead. Ctrl-C blanks the grids
and stops audio.

* Layout
#+begin_example
       x=0                                     13    14    15
y=0  | . . . . . . . . . . . . .              SHIFT SHIFT SHIFT
y=1  | . . . . . . . . . . . . .              SHIFT SHIFT SHIFT
     |
     |                   EDO PLAY SURFACE
     |
y=15 | . . . . . .  OFF  ON  TOGGLE  . . . . . . .
                   x=6  x=7  x=8
#+end_example
Everything but the shift pad and the three sustain buttons is
play surface: 41-EDO from 110 Hz, eight steps per cell rightward
and one step per cell downward. A control at rest flashes dim on
the shared 150 ms clock; a lit control is solid bright. A held
note is solid bright, and a sustained note no finger holds
flashes bright on that same clock. No dim cell circles a
sustained note: the rig turns the square dance off.

Every note is a string. A key strikes it (as a piano hammer
does), and it rings on its own decay, longer in the bass than in
the treble. Every sounding string, held or sustained, shares one
bridge with every other, so a struck note sets the others
ringing in sympathy where their partials meet, in any tuning.
The sound passes through a resonating body.

* The energy pedal: channel 1
** what it does as the energy-pedal rig's pedal does
It adds energy to a string around the moment its key goes down:
its history just before the key-down pre-charges the string, and
its position drives the string for a while after, so the note
blooms. The window around each key-down, the heel and toe, and
the exponent work as [[file:energy-pedal_monome_readme.org]]
describes under "The pedal"; the window's lengths are this rig's
'ex_ante_energizing_ms' and 'ex_post_energizing_ms'.
** it also sets how hard a key strikes
At the heel a key strikes softly, at the toe hard, in a straight
line between (the rig's target on 'exciter.pick_energy', 0.15 to
0.8). The strike is set at the key-down, so moving the pedal
afterward changes the bloom of a sounding note but not its
attack. Pressing down as you play is both a harder strike and
more energy; a soft strike with the pedal rising after it is a
quiet attack that swells.
** the window's floor
The energy table's 'ex_post_energizing_floor', 0 as shipped,
says where the window ends after a key-down. Above 0 the window
never closes, so the pedal keeps feeding every note it reached
for as long as the note sounds; high enough, a sustained note
under a held pedal grows to its ceiling and never dies on its
own. The rig gives the thresholds per register.
** before the pedal first moves
Until it first moves the pedal puts in nothing, and keys strike
at the rig's own 'pick_energy'.

* The parameters pedal: channel 2, the squeal
** at the heel, nothing
At the heel the rig sounds exactly as it would without this
pedal.
** toward the toe, feedback
As the pedal rises the strings start to feed back, like a guitar
brought close to its amplifier. Four things move together:
*** the howl's gain, from off to 20
The howl is an active loop: every sounding string, summed,
saturated, delayed a few milliseconds and fed back into every
string. Low notes catch first; by the toe every note sustains.
*** the howl's level, from 0.003 to 0.006
How loud a howling note settles: louder toward the toe, and well
under the clamp.
*** the bridge, from heavy to light
Its heaviness falls to a tenth, so the strings ring more in
sympathy and feed each other. A light bridge drains notes fast;
the howl makes up for it.
*** the tail, brighter
The upper partials ring nearly as long as the fundamental.
** what you hear
Held and sustained notes stop decaying and settle at a level of
their own, swelling as the pedal comes down. In the harness's
'squeal' scene a chord that alone falls to a quarter of its level
within two seconds instead swells past its struck level while the
pedal rises, and holds near it. Released notes still stop: the
damper wins. All four act at once on sounding notes, so rocking
the pedal back toward the heel lets them decay again.
** caution: the howl locks pitches
Among several notes the partials they share win the saturation,
which pulls near-unisons, near-fifths and near-octaves toward
just ratios. That fights the EDO tuning, the more the nearer the
toe.

* Giving a pedal other parameters
Each pedal's targets are in the rig under "pedal connection", one
'ELEM targets' headline each, with three keys: the parameter, its
value at the heel and its value at the toe. In a block like this
the headlines are indented two spaces; in the rig they start at
the margin, at the depth of the targets already there:

#+begin_example
  ***** ELEM targets
  Any notes here are for you; the loader ignores them.
  ****** PARAM parameter = "body.mix"
  ****** PARAM min = 0.3
  ****** PARAM max = 0.0
#+end_example

That one would dry the sound out as the pedal comes down: 'min'
above 'max' runs the parameter backward. 'min' equal to 'max'
pins it, a way to try a value without editing the sink. Only the
two strings pedals take targets (the energy pedal, and a pedal
whose role is 'string_parameters'). A pedal carries at most 16; a
parameter may have one target in the whole rig, so the energy
pedal's 'exciter.pick_energy' cannot also go on channel 2; and
'min' and 'max' must each be a value the parameter accepts. Only
floats the engine can change without rebuilding a table are
targetable: [[file:README.org]], "Expression pedals", "Pedal
targets", lists them. A target on an 'energy' or 'exciter' key,
or on 'modal.crossover_hz', reaches the notes struck afterward;
the rest reach the sounding strings at once. A rig that breaks a
rule fails at 'r' and the old values play on.

* Sustain
Each grid has its own sustained set. OFF, ON and TOGGLE act on
their own grid only. A sustained string keeps ringing on its own
decay, and keeps taking part in the sympathy and the howl.
** the handle: the cell below a note
Press the cell directly below a sounding note to toggle that
note's sustain. The press never sounds. Sustaining a held note
keeps it ringing after you lift your finger. Pressing the handle
of a sustained note un-sustains it, which ends it unless a
finger still holds it. The cell above a note is an ordinary
note. A note on the bottom row, or directly above OFF, ON or
TOGGLE, has no handle.
** OFF
Ends every sustained note on this grid that no finger holds.
Held notes keep sounding, now unsustained.
** ON
Notes already held, and notes played while ON is live, join the
sustained set.
** TOGGLE WHAT ON MEANS
Bright means ON is momentary: hold it. Dim means ON latches: tap
it on, tap it off. It starts bright.

* Shift
The four arrow cells move the visible register one cell. The two
outer top corners move it one octave.

* The top register, the bow and the wind
The strings are the energy-pedal rig's, so its readme's "Two

* Hot reload
Edit the rig, then press 'r' then Enter in the terminal running
it. Everything the energy-pedal readme lists under "Hot reload"
reloads the same way here. A pedal target added, removed or
re-ranged applies at once, through where the pedal is now.
'square_dance' needs a restart.

* Listening without grids
The harness renders scripted scenes with this rig's string
parameters and pedal targets to WAV files:

#+begin_src sh
cargo run -p tools --bin strings_harness -- --rig two-pedals_monome
#+end_src

Its 'squeal' scene strikes a chord and holds it while the channel
2 pedal rises from the heel at 1 s to the toe at 3 s. '--pedal
TRACE' scripts the channel 1 pedal and '--pedal2 TRACE' the
channel 2 pedal for every scene ('0:0,1:1' rises from heel to toe
over the first second); '--help' lists the rest.

* Deliberately not here
No timbre keys and no volume keys, no chord storage, no loops, no
tone-target cell, no edit mode, no SoftSteps, no square dance,
and no volume pedal: both EX-Ps are in use.
