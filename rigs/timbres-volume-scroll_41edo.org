* timbres-volume-scroll_41edo
** about
TOML-in-org format reference: [[file:README.org]]
The editor's first synth, and the plainest playable rig: one or
two 16x16 monomes, each a 41-EDO play surface with four timbre
keys, four volume keys and a scroll pad. No sustain, no pedals.
The editor ([[file:editor_readme.org]]) records and plays it; the
surfaces runtime plays it too:
=pw-jack cargo run --bin midi_pulse -- timbres-volume-scroll_41edo=

** ELEM tunings
*** PARAM id = "main"
*** PARAM edo = 41
*** PARAM x_step = 8
*** PARAM y_step = 1
*** PARAM fundamental_hz = 110
Pitch rises by 8 EDO steps to the right and 1 step downward.
Pitch 0 is 110 Hz.
