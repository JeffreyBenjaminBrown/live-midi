* 2-edogrids_ss-accrete_ss-drums
** about
This rig is written in a TOML-in-org format unique (so far) to this repo.
The data lives in headlines titled
"PARAM key = value" (values are ordinary TOML syntax); "TABLE name" / "ELEM name"
headlines nest tables and array-of-table elements; any other headline just groups and
documents; and every body (like this one) is a human note the loader ignores. A "#" on
a headline starts a comment, TOML-style, even on a load-bearing one. Format reference
(C-c C-o to follow): [[file:README.org]]
Reference doc for this rig: [[file:2-edogrids_ss-accrete_ss-drums_readme.org]]

*** PARAM id = "2-edogrids_ss-accrete_ss-drums"
*** PARAM title = "Two EDO grids + an accrete SoftStep + a drum SoftStep"
** shared SoftStep parameters (softstep.toml)
This rig drives at least one SoftStep board. Every board reads its detection and
pressure parameters from the shared file rigs/softstep.toml, not from this rig
file. That shared file holds the onset and release thresholds, the attack window,
the pressure scaling, the contact-bounce and settle debounce guards, and the
de-stick window. Every rig that uses a SoftStep reads the same file, and the
meter reads it too, so one edit there reaches all of them at once.
** about the rig
===========================================================================
FOUR SURFACES, WITH DRUMS. The drum-surface twin of
2-edogrids_ss-accrete_ss-pulse: identical in every respect except what the
NEWER SoftStep does. Two 16x16 monome grids, each a full 46-EDO play
surface; the OLDER board (SSCOM) still carries sustain and edit mode, two
rows as ever; and the NEWER board (SoftStep) is now a velocity-sensitive
TR-808 drumkit instead of the factored-pulse pedals.
Jeff never actually touched those pulse pedals -- and their whole function
also lives on each grid's own upper-right 3x2 pad -- so this trades a
duplicate control surface for a kit. Nothing is lost: the factored pulse is
still fully drivable by hand.
Each grid keeps the same overlays -- a 4-cell waveform strip (top-left), a
chord block, an editmode-clear button at (12,0), a 3x2 factored-pulse pad
(top-right, now the ONLY pulse surface), a fine-transpose toggle at (0,15),
and a 3x2 scroll pad (bottom-right). Everything else on the grid is a note.
Launch under pw-jack so the synth AND the drum sampler share the card:
pw-jack cargo run --bin midi_pulse -- 2-edogrids_ss-accrete_ss-drums

** TABLE trail
Shared recent-note trail (the dim backdrop of the last few played pitch classes).
*** PARAM clobber_radius = 27
Clobber radius as a divisor of the octave: a played note clears any trailed class
within edo/clobber_radius steps of it. 27 -> 1/27 of an octave (~44 cents).
*** PARAM max = 9
At most this many distinct pitch classes in the trail (newest first).
** TABLE slide
*** PARAM candidate_window_ms = 1000
Slide: a released note stays a slide source for this long.
*** PARAM duration_ms = 100
How long a glide takes. ALSO the per-voice pitch-edit drag time: dragging an
edit-mode note to a new pitch reuses this, so 'r' retunes the drag feel live.
Jeff's starting value; slow enough to hear it move, fast enough not to smear
the rhythm.
*** PARAM pedal_smoother_ms = 30
Pedal slide (TODO/pedal-slide): the one-pole time constant that smooths the
EX-P pedal's CC steps into a continuous pitch glide. Bigger = softer/laggier.
1_vision first tried 10; 30 is Jeff's softened value. Hot-reloadable ('r').
** TABLE tap_tempo
*** PARAM window_ms = 2000
Two taps at most this far apart on a pad's tap cell set the one global tempo.
The base is seeded at 1 Hz, so tapping is optional: the tap pedal was retired
(Jeff never tapped), and the on-grid pads' tap cells are the only tap source.

** the two softsteps
Distinguished by ALSA client name, which is reliable: the older board presents
as "SSCOM", the newer as "SoftStep". Their CARD numbers follow plug order and
are NOT stable, so nothing here names one.
The older board keeps its sibling-rig job (sustain + edit mode); the newer one
is the drumkit. Either may be unplugged on its own: the runtime brings up every
board that IS present and names the missing one in red, so the drums still play
with the sustain board absent, and vice versa.
*** old softstep: sustain (bottom row) + edit mode (top row)
Two rows. Bottom row = the SUSTAIN bank: outer pedals (1/5) clear it, inner
pedals (2/4) accrete into it, momentary only (hold to add, release to stop
adding). Top row = EDIT MODE, same button shape: outer pedals (6/0) clear it,
inner pedals (7/9) accrete into it (a one-shot: every sounding voice enters
edit mode). The accretes capture the same voices: sustain-accrete takes every
fingered AND every edit-mode voice; editmode-accrete takes every fingered AND
every sustained voice (sustaining any it finds only fingered). But the two
CLEARS are NOT symmetric: edit is a selection over the sustained voices, not a
reason to ring (edited ⊆ sustained). The sustain clear ends every fingerless
drone -- edited or not -- and deselects it; the edit clear only deselects and
silences nothing. (This supersedes the earlier symmetric-clears design, where
each clear removed only its own reason and the full kill was both together.)
Left buttons drive the left grid, right buttons the right grid.
The tap pedal that once sat here was retired; the base tempo is a seeded 1 Hz.

*** new softstep: the drumkit
The velocity-sensitive TR-808 kit from 2-monomes_kmss-drums, moved onto THIS
board because Jeff never touched the factored-pulse pedals that used to live
here -- and did not need to, since every one of them is duplicated on the
grids' own upper-right pads. So the pedals are simply gone from this rig; the
factored pulse is unchanged and entirely hand-driven.
Pressure-sensitive, not MIDI-velocity: the runtime tethers the board on start
(SysEx over rawmidi), reads each pad's four sensors as CC 40..79, sums them
into a pressure 0..1 and spreads that over softstep.toml's gain_db_range.
Standalone mode is restored on exit or Ctrl-C.

The pedal NUMBERS differ from 2-monomes_kmss-drums even though the kit is the
same, and that is the point. That rig put the drums on the OLDER board, which
is not rotated; this board is turned 180 degrees, so binding the same labels
would land every drum in the mirror-opposite place. These numbers are chosen so
each drum sits where Jeff's foot already expects it. Physically, as he stands:
  far row  (5 4 3 2 1) : high_tom  low_tom  wood_block  cowbell  crash
  near row (0 9 8 7 6) : snare     kick     ditto       hat      open_hat
Ditto (label 8 here, label 3 there) keeps its old spot too: middle of the NEAR
row, in the gap between the feet, where either foot can reach it. It is not a
sample -- it retriggers whichever sample most recently played on any pedal in
this window, at THIS press's pressure against that sample's own pad's gain
trim, "a generalized double-bass pedal". A ditto press never itself becomes
"the last hit", so pad, ditto, ditto repeats the sample rather than echoing the
ditto. It does nothing until something else has played.
Pedal 1's crash is the synthesized crash_electronic.wav (not the 808 CY), which
is fuller than the rest of the kit -- hence the 0.5 trim.
Detection/velocity knobs (debounce, de-stick, thresholds) are shared and live
in rigs/softstep.toml, not here.
Sample files are NOT checked when the rig parses (so rigs stay unit-testable
without a filesystem); a missing WAV errors at launch instead. They resolve
under the top-level drum-samples/ folder, and a sample may name a subpath if a
second kit ever lands there.
** the EX-P volume pedals
Two M-Audio EX-P expression pedals, reaching the runtime via the MPC-20 ->
host bridge (run tools/pedals/bridge.sh on the HOST; if its
"MPC-20 pedals" port is missing the runtime prints a red note and plays on
without them). Each is a volume pedal for one grid: the pedal's reliable
~1..119 CC travel maps to a position 0..1, tapered by the fader law -- an
exponential (dB-linear) over most of the travel, spliced to a linear fade at
the heel; see the curve_* params on each pedal below -- full heel silent,
full toe unity -- applied to that grid's sounding and future voices, slewed
in the engine (~20 ms) so sweeps don't zipper. Until a pedal first moves it
contributes unity, so an unplugged pedal never mutes its grid.
Swap the monome values here if the pedals end up cabled the other way round.
*** ELEM expression_pedals            # MPC-20 channel 1 -> grid A's volume
**** PARAM channel = 1
**** PARAM monome = "a"
The taper (both params at their defaults, spelled out so the names are here):
the top (1 - curve_initial_lin_frac) of the travel is EXPONENTIAL, running
dB-linearly over curve_remainder_exp_db up to unity at full toe; the first
curve_initial_lin_frac fades LINEARLY from exact silence up to the
exponential's floor, since an exponential alone never reaches 0.
**** PARAM curve_initial_lin_frac = 0.1
**** PARAM curve_remainder_exp_db = 50.0
*** ELEM expression_pedals            # MPC-20 channel 2 -> grid B's volume
**** PARAM channel = 2
**** PARAM monome = "b"
**** PARAM curve_initial_lin_frac = 0.1
**** PARAM curve_remainder_exp_db = 50.0
