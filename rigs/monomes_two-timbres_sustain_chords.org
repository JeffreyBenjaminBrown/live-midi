** identity
*** PARAM version = 1
*** PARAM id = "monomes_two-timbres_sustain_chords"
*** PARAM title = "Monomes: sustain, chords, and compact loops"
** tuning
*** ELEM tunings
**** PARAM id = "main"
**** PARAM edo = 41
**** PARAM x_step = 8
**** PARAM y_step = 1
**** PARAM fundamental_hz = 110
Pitch rises by 8 EDO steps to the right and 1 step downward. Pitch 0 is 110 Hz.
** motion
*** TABLE slide
**** PARAM candidate_window_ms = 1000
Unused by this rig, but retained as the shared slide table's ordinary default.
**** PARAM duration_ms = 100
The octave corners use this glide time when moving held chord voices.
**** PARAM pedal_smoother_ms = 30
Unused: this rig has no expression pedals.
** audio
*** ELEM sinks
**** PARAM id = "synth"
**** PARAM kind = "cpal_synth"
**** PARAM sample_rate = 48000
**** PARAM buffer_frames = 128
**** PARAM amplitude = 0.15
**** PARAM attack_secs = 0.003
**** PARAM release_secs = 0.050
**** PARAM sustain_level = 0.35
**** PARAM decay_secs = 0.5
**** PARAM crowded_pitch_planck_deviation = 5
The waveform selector uses the built-in sine / triangle / square / saw choices.
Loudness belongs to the tone controls below, initially -12 dB.  Here “tone”
means the combined timbre and volume setting.
Equal-nominal-pitch surface voices take the first free lane in
=0,+p,-p,+2p,-2p,...=, where this parameter is =p= in cents.  Set it to zero to
disable crowded-pitch separation.
** grid A: the first available 16x16 monome
*** ELEM monomes
**** PARAM id = "a"
**** PARAM listen_port = 9000
**** PARAM prefix = "/two-timbres-a"
**** PARAM select.size = [16, 16]
No serial pin: whichever matching monome serialosc reports first may fill A.
The persistent chord space is still keyed by the physical serial id.
