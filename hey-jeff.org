* Glossary
** Standard :: Standard
is the debounce mode that every pad used before this work,
and it is the mode that most pads still use. A pad in Standard mode fires the
moment its pad sum crosses the on-threshold. It then watches for a louder peak
through its amplitude window. It re-arms once its pad sum falls below the
off-threshold. It also refuses to re-fire within a short fixed min-gap after its
last fire. This mode suits a drum pad, because a fast double-tap on a drum is
two real hits. It also suits a held pedal such as an accrete pedal, because
every genuine press counts and a lift must register at once.
** Settle
is the debounce mode for the timing pedals. A pad in Settle mode
fires once, and it then emits no louder-peak updates. It refuses to fire again
until two conditions both hold. The first condition is that at least 150
milliseconds have passed since the fire. The second condition is that the pad has
been inactive for the last 25 milliseconds, which means that its pad sum has
stayed below the off-threshold for that whole span. A single stomp makes the pad
sum rock above and below the off-threshold, and each rise restarts the inactivity
clock, so a bouncing stomp never satisfies the second condition and produces
exactly one event.
*** DONE this two-condition rule is now live
I replaced the old steady-for-150-ms rule with your two conditions. The two
numbers are =factor_settle_ms= (150) and =factor_release_ms= (25) in
=rigs/softstep.toml=. After a long hold the pad now re-arms about 25 milliseconds
after the foot lifts, rather than 150 milliseconds later, so quick repeats stay
responsive.
** de-stick
De-stick is the rule that a sensor which has sent no reading for a
set time reads as zero. This rule frees a pad whose sensor has physically stuck.
The set time is 25 milliseconds today.

*** timing pedal
is one of the four tempo-factor pedals, which are
the =x2=, =x3=, =/2=, and =/3= pedals. Each one multiplies a grid's tempo
factor. I gave these pedals the Settle debounce. Some people also call these the
factor pedals.
*** =1 pedal
is the pedal that switches a grid's factored pulse on
and off. The word Unity also names this pedal in the code. This pedal is not a
timing pedal, and it runs its own double-tap dance.
*** double-tap dance
double-tap dance is the sequence of =1 presses that
turns a grid's factored pulse off. A first press snaps the tempo factor to
unity, and a second press within 400 milliseconds turns the factored pulse off.
*** accrete pedal
accrete pedal is a softstep pedal that sustains notes. A
musician holds it to add notes to a grid's sustained set, and the musician
releases it to stop adding notes.
*** drum pad
A drum pad is a softstep pad that plays a drum sample when a foot
strikes it. No rig uses drum pads at the moment, though the code still supports
them.
*** pad sum
A pad sum is the sum of a pad's four pressure sensors. The decoder
compares this pad sum against the on-threshold and the off-threshold.
*** amplitude window
The amplitude window is the short span after a pad fires,
during which a louder peak can raise the note's volume. This window lasts about
14 milliseconds by default.
*** the meter
The meter is the =softstep_meter= program. It shows every pad's raw
pressure so that you can see what the hardware is sending. It reads the same
=rigs/softstep.toml= file that the runtime reads.
*** monomekey
A monomekey is one button on a monome grid. A monomekey sends a
clean press and a clean release, and it carries no pressure reading.
